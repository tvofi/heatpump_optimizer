ci_critpath --drop-edge (R10-FLOW-TOOLKIT-MEASURE, #2645): a window that straddles a job-graph change refuses only when both jobs of the dropped edge still run. If the change renames or removes either job, the drop is a silent no-op (gain 0, rc=0) and the median mixes two graphs. Read the window over runs on one side of the change only, using --run lists or --event/--branch, and check that the job names match at both ends of the window before quoting a W figure.
