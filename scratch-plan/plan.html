How the ETAs are computed

Every figure on this page is plan.json's, written by tools/plan/replan.py; this renderer draws it and computes no schedule of its own. A wave is the set of groups dispatched at one moment, and the next opens as soon as one of 6 pull-request slots frees, so a wave never waits for the previous one to drain; the cap limits concurrency, not the wave boundary. Duration is dispatch to merge by roster size, as the plan recorded it from tools/plan/durations.json: S 13.1 h, M 11.4 h, L 20.1 h, XL 30.2 h. median (latest merge - earliest PR commit) over 25 done round-10 groups: S n=16, M n=7, L n=2; XL = 1.5 x L because no XL group has merged. Wall-clock including review rounds and queueing, so a group that waited in review inflates its size (S 13.1 > M 11.4). Measured in wave-data.md section 3 at 2026-10-18; no merged group has changed the method since. A group whose fixer is already working gets 70% of its size duration as the remaining estimate. Those are measured lead times, not effort: review rounds and queueing are inside them, so the slot schedule is an upper bound on closure. The batching window is 0 minutes; its measured trade is in durations.json.
