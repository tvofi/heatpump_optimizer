1. The answer

The process does not converge on lead time or token use, within round 10 or across rounds 8 to 10. On quality it removes product defects faster than it injects them, but the injection rate is rising and nothing measures escapes per round, so no quality trend can be drawn. None of the three has all four parts of a control loop: a target, a sensor, feedback faster than the disturbance, and a controller with bounded cost.
