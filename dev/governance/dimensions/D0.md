# D0 — Price optimality

Owner's brief: *"Price optimality. Specifically hunt for sub-optimal planning."*

Read `COMMON.md` first: it says what counts.

## What you are hunting

Plans the shipped solver returns that a better search finds strictly cheaper
on the **same objective, bounds and inputs**, with comfort and hot-water
feasibility no worse. Three known homes: seeding and multi-start (which basin
the starts land in), the budget (iterations, evaluations, tolerance), and
structure the solver cannot see (bang-bang schedules, the DHW/space
decomposition, the terminal credit, horizon effects).
Whether the objective is the right one is D2's; you ask whether the solver
minimises the one it has.

## Method

1. **Capture harness.** `mock.patch.object(optimizer_module, "_multi_start_minimize", ...)`
   (the idiom in `tests/optimality.py`) to record every call's objective,
   candidates, bounds, args, `maxiter` and result, then race challengers
   against it. Score every plan with the production objective, never energy
   price alone: cheaper energy with worse comfort pull or cycling is not cheaper.
2. **Challengers**, cheapest first: more L-BFGS-B starts (structured seeds:
   bang-bang at several energy fractions, greedy-cheapest-hours, the
   thermostat baseline, the previous cell's plan); larger `maxiter` and
   `maxfun`; a different `eps`; warm-starting from the best challenger; a
   global method (`scipy.optimize.differential_evolution` or a coarse-grid
   dynamic program) as an outer bound on the same objective. Report the gap
   from production to the best feasible challenger and to the outer bound.
3. **Feasibility parity** before any "cheaper" verdict: per-step comfort floor
   and ceiling, DHW availability in windows, power bounds, manual pins.
4. **The grid**: every price × weather profile in `tests/profiles.py`,
   single/two-zone, DHW on/off, valve storage, wood, horizon 6/24/48 h, and
   the coordinator captures. Report per cell; the aggregate needs
   leave-one-out and the most-favourable-cell drop.
5. **Null control**: the same race at `profiles.prices("flat")`. A gap that
   survives flat prices is not price optimality; name what it is. A real
   finding that is out of dimension is carried per `finding-propagation.md`.
6. **MPC masking**: per gap cell, does the step-0 action differ? If
   re-planning reaches the same trajectory the gap is not realised; simulate
   a day's receding horizon (`tests/rolling.py:run_rolling` shape).
7. **Decomposition**: does iterating DHW ↔ space again change cost? Does the
   terminal credit act as its docstring claims?

## Reuse

`tests/optimality.py` (`setup`, `evaluate`), `tests/golden.py:make`,

## Traps

- Timing under the fan-out is provisional; count evaluations and iterations.
- A tighter stop (`ftol`, on all runs or only the winner) is refused on money
  (#1293; #2612: non-flat +0.15 %, flat null -0.79 %, 1 of 6 cells worse).
  Show realised closed-loop money that does not fall on flat, in
  `r10_solver_winner_polish.py`'s shape, or it duplicates #1293.
- `_solve_space`'s DHW seeds are unguarded: `_CERT_CELLS` holds space-only
  cells, so add DHW cells there before reading a seed gap as closed.
- The batched gradient serves some bound shapes only: race with the `jac`
  path production uses (capture it).
- `_MULTI_START_SOLVES` and pre-refinement scoring decide which candidates
  get refined; count what is discarded.
- Report SEK/day beside the flat-price arm and the bill share: 1 % of a 60 SEK
  day is a finding, of a 2 SEK summer day not.
