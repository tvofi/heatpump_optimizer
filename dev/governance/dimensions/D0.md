# D0 — Price optimality

Owner's brief: *"Price optimality. Specifically hunt for sub-optimal planning."*

Read `COMMON.md` first.

## What you are hunting

Plans the shipped solver returns that a better search finds strictly cheaper
on the **same objective, bounds and inputs**, with comfort and hot-water
feasibility no worse. Sub-optimality has three known homes: the seeding and
multi-start (which basin the starts land in), the budget (iterations, function
evaluations, tolerance), and structure the solver cannot see (bang-bang
schedules, the DHW/space decomposition, the terminal credit, horizon effects).
Your modules are `python3 tools/audit/enumerate.py d0-modules`, the solver's
import closure, not `optimizer.py` alone.

## Method

1. **Capture harness.** `mock.patch.object(optimizer_module, "_multi_start_minimize", ...)`
   (the idiom in `tests/optimality.py`) to record every call's objective,
   candidates, bounds, args, `maxiter` and result, then race challengers
   against the exact recorded objective. Score every plan with the production
   objective, never with energy price alone: a challenger that is cheaper on
   energy and worse on comfort pull or cycling is not cheaper.
2. **Challengers**, every one per cell, cheapest first: more L-BFGS-B starts (structured seeds:
   bang-bang at several energy fractions, greedy-cheapest-hours, the
   thermostat baseline, the previous cell's plan); larger `maxiter` and
   `maxfun`; a different `eps`; warm-starting L-BFGS-B from
   the best challenger; a global method (`scipy.optimize.differential_evolution`
   or a dynamic program on a coarsened grid) as an outer bound on the same
   objective. Report the gap between production and the best feasible
   challenger, and the gap to the outer bound.
3. **Feasibility parity** before any "cheaper" verdict: per-step comfort floor
   and ceiling, DHW availability in windows, power bounds, manual pins.
4. **The grid**, whole: `enumerate.py d0-grid` prints every cell (price ×
   weather profile of `tests/profiles.py`, topology, DHW, valve storage or
   wood, horizon), plus the coordinator captures. Every round races the
   price × weather plane; `rotation.json`'s rotation carries the other
   planes by name. Report per cell; the aggregate needs leave-one-out and
   the most-favourable-cell drop.
5. **Null control**: the same race at `profiles.prices("flat")`. A gap that
   survives flat prices is not price optimality; name what it is. A real
   finding that is out of dimension is carried per `finding-propagation.md`.
6. **MPC masking**: for every cell with a gap, does the step-0 action differ?
   If re-planning at the next step reaches the same trajectory, the gap is
   not realised.
7. **Decomposition**: does iterating DHW ↔ space more than once change cost?
   Does the terminal credit change the plan the way its docstring claims?

## Reuse

`tests/optimality.py` (`setup`, the patch idiom), `tests/golden.py:make`
and `SCENARIOS`, `tests/backtest.py:score` and its strategies,
`tests/stress.py:build_case`, `tests/profiles.py`.

## Traps

- The batched gradient serves some bound shapes and not others; a race
  must use the same `jac` path production would (capture it).
- A stop-tolerance change is refused on money (#1293), the winner-only polish
  too (#2612: non-flat +0.15 %, flat null -0.79 %, 1 of 6 cells worse). A
  challenger that tightens the stop duplicates #1293 unless it shows realised
  closed-loop money that does not fall on a flat price, in
  `r10_solver_winner_polish.py`'s shape. Re-measure at your merge base.
- `_solve_space`'s DHW seed set is unguarded: `_CERT_CELLS` holds space-only
  cells, so add DHW cells there before reading a seed gap as closed.
- Report SEK/day only beside the flat-price arm and the bill share; a 1 %
  gap on a 60 SEK day is a finding, a 1 % gap on a 2 SEK summer day is not.
