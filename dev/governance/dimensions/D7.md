# D7 — Architecture and maintainability

Owner's brief: *"Architecture and maintainability — explicitly revisit
coordinator.py's size, the first-order sysid plant, the learner-freeze-versus-COP-flow
item, last_buffer_trajectory statefulness, and everything this year's train
introduced."*

Read `COMMON.md` first. Report counts and ratios; never timing.

## Method

1. **Metrics are the ratchet's**: `tests/structure.py` measures every key of
   `tests/structure_budgets.json`. Report a metric it lacks, or a reading.
2. **Sysid plant.** Step responses of `ThermalModel`'s three presets, fitted
   first-order (production) and two-exponential: each UA bias, and whether
   the adoption gate admits the biased fit.
3. **Learner freeze.** Each learner against the signal gating its samples
   (window, pumps, external heat, defrost, away): is a contaminated
   interval ingested?
4. **Implied order.** What a reordering or a second objective call does to
   the terminal cost through `last_buffer_trajectory`. `CoordinatorContext`
   is documented write-once, yet `async_update_thermal_params` replaces it:
   what does a holder of the old one see?
5. **This year's train.** Each addition since v4 (batched gradient, drift and
   scoped gates, card collaborators, stress budgets, weekly windows, topology
   catalogue, wood, coil, DHW confidence, Tuya, re-anchor law): a spot
   mutation a test fails on, a doc paragraph, one owner.
6. **Dead code** by AST reachability, a runtime sentinel where a dynamic
   lookup could hide a use.
7. **Arch-score opportunities.** `tools/audit/archscore/score.py`, every
   counter on, per prototype and combined: admissible, behaviour suite green
   with no check lost, an architectural argument, 0.0000 reverted. Guideline
   bar: 150 % of the cells' 30 % share. A review trigger, never a target: a
   gain one of its documented blind spots explains is void; a clone-copy drop
   counts only where the copies are gone. Cells at their floor test
   their counters; a hole is a finding.
8. **Duplicated shapes.** Per rule written twice, enumerate implementations by
   a seam rule written as code, drive all with the same probes (an agreement
   test), report disagreements, name one owner. `shape_copies` is a floor
   below the clone window, never the census.
