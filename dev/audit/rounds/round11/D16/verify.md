## Attacks and notes, per finding

- **s1-01.** Leave-one-out over ranges is a grid artefact. The claim "every heating state" holds only on ranges where span/20 is not an integer: 4 of my 8 ranges give 0 off-grid writes. The half-hertz error is far below FREQ_DIVERGENCE_HZ = 5, so the watchdog never trips; the device rounds or truncates by at most 0.5 Hz. The phenomenon is real (the set-point writers quantise, the frequency writer does not), but the consequence is small, hence low.
- **s1-02.** Reachable only when a GCHV tank sits more than 5 K under 30 C, i.e. below 25 C: a cold start, after a holiday, or a fresh install. The class doc states the design ("never below DHW_GATE_C"), so the finding is the doc's floor against the D16 baseline row. The 30 C floor is a one-time charge and recurs only while the tank stays under 25 C, hence low.
- **s1-03.** Both arms reproduce. The consequence is that the user-requested space boost does nothing on indoor-set-point units. Supply and mode still lift, so heat reaches the room only if the room is already under the plan's set-point. It is a comfort miss with no frost or money risk, hence medium.
- **s1-04.** Of the 13 findings this has the strongest consequence. The coordinator's comment promises "the pump's own weather-compensated curve holds comfort", yet the switch it turned off stays off for as long as the solves fail. My run gives 240 of 240 minutes; the null is 0.
- **s1-05.** The tick after recovery rewrites the mode, so the cost is one tick plus a phantom duty_switch mode write.
- **s2-01.** **My null control fails for the frequency arm.** At `--interval 15 --phases 0` the frequency share reads 0.1818 (28 of 154 graded minutes on the both and partial steps), on this tree and on the baseline tree. The finder recorded 0. The residual does not depend on cycle phase, so it is not the boundary mechanism; it is likely the 5-minute rate limit or the 1 Hz dedup. The mqtt and curve arms pass the null with 0 and reproduce exactly. V2 confirms the mechanism: across the boundary the arbiter re-derives its write and the frequency and mqtt writers do not. I weakened the finding because its frequency share needs the null subtracted, about 0.45 - 0.18. Disputed number: frequency null 0.1818 against a reported 0.
- **s2-02.** Every number reproduces exactly. The tank set-point still reaches 60 C inside the window (`dhw_setpoint_at_target_minutes_in_window` = 29), so hygiene is met. The consequence is the pump's own disinfection program running one interval late, outside the booked (cheap) step, plus the wrong mode inside it. I rate that medium, not high.
- **s2-03.** Corrected to P2. `hold()` drops a record the entity no longer reads; `_lead_mode` trusts the record without that check. The seam_rule greps only the `_differs` skip. The other record-trusting writers (frequency watchdog dedup, `_pump_commanded`, `_valve_commanded_target`) are not enumerated.
- **s2-04.** The plant is the finder's synthetic first-order model. The production property, that the loop folds only running samples and `_note_stop` learns nothing at the base target, is confirmed in code. Reach depends on how far a real plant's lift departs from the map.
- **s3-01.** The module docstring and tests/features.py pin "nothing else" on Off. The D16 brief's off row (owner's 2026-11-15 ruling) requires every written entity back to its baseline. That is a contract conflict: the fix changes pinned behaviour, so the owner must confirm it. On the fork, the pump is left in DHW-only after Off, with no space heating. High is earned.
- **s3-02.** A pump switched off on an idle step stays off after Off, after unload, and after removal, when no later setup can repair it. High is earned.
- **s3-03.** A tank left at 60 C instead of 50 C costs energy; 60 C is a common standard set-point, so this is no safety issue. Medium.
- **s3-04.** Reproduced. The perturbation moves the number up, as stated.
