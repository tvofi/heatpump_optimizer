## 2. Named cause

**`harness` is a parent verdict class, and the friction key counts the parent.** `VERDICT_CLASSES` in
`.claude/workflows/web-fix-wave.js` has one word, `harness`. `fix-review.md` hangs at least
three distinct obligations under it, each with its own sub-reason: step 6 (`class-open`,
`class-rule-missing`), step 9 (`design-trace-missing`, #1588), and the finding harness being
absent or un-re-runnable. `policy_lint.mjs`'s histogram keys on the class alone.

The previous filing of this key, #1881 (window v6.7.14), was triaged as the third obligation. It
was closed FIXED on 2026-10-04 because `e3860e5b` (#1879, fixer.md step 18, harnesses land in
`tools/audit/harnesses/`) held the next window to 2 PRs. That countermeasure still holds: none
of the seven entries above is a missing or out-of-tree harness. The key recurred through the
other obligations, and the class word cannot tell them apart.

Per sub-reason:

- **`class-open` (4 entries, 3 PRs) is the only sub-reason at threshold.** The fixer contract
  and the reviewer contract disagree on when a class enumeration is owed.
  - `fixer.md` step 8 makes the enumeration conditional. It applies "If the block you are
    clearing was demonstrated with an *instance*". Step 3 defers to it ("The class's seams and
    the rule that enumerates them are step 8's").
  - `fix-review.md` step 6 runs the class on every review, round 1 included.
  - Entries 2 and 4 were round-1 reviews with no earlier block. The bodies (`#1986` body edit
    of 10:31Z, `handoff-body/r9-ro-7` at `e5ae0a91`) name no enumeration command. The fixer
    contract, as worded, did not ask them for one.
  - Entry 3 (#1983, round 4) clears no instance block either; its earlier blocks were `claims`.
    Its body (`handoff-body/r9-diag-2s` at `bf4ee83c`) does name a rule:
    `rg -n "_apply_house_heat_loss_scale" .../coordinator.py`. It summarises the result instead
    of listing every returned seam.
  - Entry 6 is the only one where step 8 bound. It was clearing entry 4. The seam it missed
    arrived from `main` after the measurement: `3725f062`, merge parent `1b1bbaad`. The fixer's
    own committed rule (`tests/harness_headers.py` `inline_scan`) printed that seam at the
    blocked head, and the body still said it "exits 0".
- **`design-trace-missing` (1)**: a judge-design requirement that no test exercised (#1960).
- **`since-null` (1)**: an R9-RO-1 carry, "set `since` to the pull-request number for each
