## 2. Named cause

**`harness` is a parent verdict class, and the friction key counts the parent.** `VERDICT_CLASSES` in
`.claude/workflows/web-fix-wave.js` has one word, `harness`. `fix-review.md` hangs at least
three distinct obligations under it, each with its own sub-reason: step 6 (`class-open`,
`class-rule-missing`), step 9 (`design-trace-missing`, #1588), and the finding harness being
absent or un-re-runnable. `policy_lint.mjs`'s histogram keys on the class alone.

The previous filing of this key, #1881 (window v6.7.14), was triaged as the third obligation. It
was closed FIXED on 2026-10-04 because `e3860e5b` (#1879, fixer.md step 18, harnesses land in
`tools/audit/harnesses/`) held the next window to 2 PRs. That countermeasure still holds: none
of the seven entries above is a missing or out-of-tree harness. The key recurred through the
other obligations, and the class word cannot tell them apart.

Per sub-reason:
