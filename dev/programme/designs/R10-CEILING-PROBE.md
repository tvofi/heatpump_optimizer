## Owning seam

`sysid.py` owns when the experiment runs and what it commands, `draw_range.py` what a sample teaches the band, `pump_arbiter.py` how an action becomes a set-point, `coordinator.py` the wiring. One path: `arm(kind)` sizes the step, `boost.adopt_plan` carries it as the action, the arbiter turns the action's power into a level, and the metered draw with the written set-point — both the interval's own — reaches `draw_range.fold`, which feeds the window and the ceiling store, whose median `diagnostics_view` publishes.

## 1. One sized variable per kind (revise 1)

- **The fit keeps the sizer.** `_begin_step_phase` sets `_step_power` from `_size_step_power` (`sysid.py:1547`, `sysid.py:1564`); that sizer returns `max_power_kw` when the full step fits and bisects below otherwise (`sysid.py:1469-1470`). Its answer is the house's, not a constant (F1).
- **The probe's bound is a duration search.** At q = p_max × COP, `_predict_step_excursion_plant` runs over candidate durations, longest first, in whole cycles (`DEFAULT_OPTIMIZATION_INTERVAL`, `const.py:1423`), against the sizer's own test; below two cycles the arm refuses. `_unadoptable` (`sysid.py:1564-1566`, where the step power is fixed) is the fit's alone: a probe adopts nothing (F2).
- **The arbiter reads the action's power, not the plan's.** `_planned_duty` returns `space` while that power exceeds 0.05 kW and `idle` otherwise — the threshold `heat_pump_on` already uses (`coordinator.py:10989`). The space path of `_flow_target` (`pump_arbiter.py:613-623`) maps it through a level function in place of the plan's `step_duty` (`pump_arbiter.py:325-359`); the mapping sends p_max to the heat level, so the ceiling is not a branch (F3).
- **Invariant.** The action's `power` equals what the step phase returns (`sysid.py:1698`, `_step_power`), the level the arbiter maps, and the power the bound was computed at; duty is `space` exactly when the heat pump is on. The booking already writes that power and its normalized form (`coordinator.py:10985-10991`).
- **Each kind has its own clock.** `arm` tests that kind's interval: the fit keeps `min_days_between_runs` (`sysid.py:1365-1374`); the probe owns 7 days unlearned, 30 once learned. One helper, `armable(...) -> (kind, refusal)`, serves `arm` and the button predicate. Probe eligibility is install-level: a meter configured and a flow set-point driven (the conditions `_flow_command` tests, `pump_arbiter.py:563-581`, less the written record), because the command is unset before the first pass.

## 3. The store: feed point, reader, latch (re-cut A, B)

- **Re-cut A — the feed point is the interval's own.** The store rides the one `draw_range.fold`, and that fold's ask and command must be the interval's own; at the base they are not. `_record_accuracy` (`coordinator.py:5861`, its fold at `9841`) runs after `async_run_optimization` (`5826`, whose experiment override is adopted at `10984`) and after `_apply_action` (`5845`, whose `pump_arbiter.apply` at `7558` has just written the set-point), so `_commanded_split` (`4695`) and `pump_arbiter.flow_command` (`551`) describe the **next** interval while `_measured_power` (`6453`) is the closed one's draw. The pair is therefore captured where it is written — after `_apply_action` — and the fold consumes the previous cycle's capture: the rule the coordinator already states for the house learner (`coordinator.py:5430-5433`: the action governing the elapsed interval is the one `_current_action` holds before this cycle's solve replaces it). The fold is not moved earlier instead: `defrost_window = self._defrost_window.close(now)` (`coordinator.py:9829`) must run on every cycle and is what the fold's `defrost` argument is. Measured (feed probe): the live pairing stores a 2.0 h step as 4 samples `[1.5, 3.0, 3.0, 3.0]` and a 1.0 h step as 2 `[1.5, 3.0]` — the settling interval's 1.5 kW plan draw booked at the step's p_max ask; twelve samples are 3 runs at 2.0 h (median 3.0) but 6 at 1.0 h (**median 2.25 kW** for a 3.0 kW pump, under the `0.85 × p_max` bar of 4.25, so it adopts). With the interval's own pair: 4 samples at 3.0 kW and 2 at 3.0 kW; twelve are 3 runs at 2.0 h or 6 at 1.0 h, median 3.0 in both (F9). The window folds with the same pair, so its ask is corrected with the store's; its own filters are untouched, and `tests/features.py:65457-65490` drives `fold` with matched pairs and does not move.
- **Re-cut B — the routing.** An interval the experiment commanded (`_current_action["mode"] == "system_identification"`, `coordinator.py:10990`) whose written set-point is the ceiling (`CEILING_TOLERANCE_K`, `draw_range.py:138`) and which passes `is_running_space` (`draw_range.py:372`) and `_evidence`'s above-idle rule (`draw_range.py:125-145`) goes to a second deque, `ceiling`, on the same `DrawRange`, and **not** to `samples`; every other interval folds into the window exactly as today. The separate deque is not tidiness: `_disagrees` runs after every append (`draw_range.py:190-211`) and `engaged` is sticky, so 48 ceiling samples first on an empty window latch at append 48 and the final share (0.143 at 336) never releases it; a 48-sample window of ceiling samples alone reads `engaged=True, planned=(3.0, 3.0)`, the plan clamped to the experiment's own measurement. With the routing no probe sample reaches `observe`, so a probe cannot trip the latch (F6, F7).
- **Re-cut B — the reader.** The accessor is a new read, `learned_max_kw()`, and `diagnostics_view` (`draw_range.py:464-470`) publishes it beside `effective_max_kw`, outside the `engaged` gate. At the base nothing reads a store: `_metered_range` reads `observed()`, the window only (`draw_range.py:162-172`, `147`); it is reached only from `planned` (`draw_range.py:217-226`, `None` unless `engaged`), and `planned_range` only from `diagnostics_view` (`draw_range.py:438`, `469`). Measured: the note's F6 case reads `observed=(1.5, 3.0)` but `planned=None` and `effective_max_kw=None`; twelve samples in the window leave the high `None` (48 are needed, `draw_range.py:159`); a store-only twelve reads `observed=None`, `effective_max_kw=None` (F10). `learned_max_kw()` is the median of `ceiling`, or `None` below twelve samples (`CEILING_MIN_SAMPLES`) and 45 days after the newest one (`CEILING_LAPSE_DAYS`); the deque and the newest sample's day persist in `as_dict`/`from_dict`. Replacing the configured top with it is `_metered_range`'s own test (`draw_range.py:162-172`) and no consumer here does it, which is why the plan cannot move. Spike (this seat's, uncommitted): eleven store samples → `None`, twelve → 3.0, 48 → 3.0 with `engaged=False`, `observed=None`, `effective_max_kw=None`; day 46 → `None`; twelve at 4.5 kW → `None` (F11).
- **Learning only.** Nothing in the solve reads the learned range: `planned_range` is called only by `diagnostics_view` (`draw_range.py:438`, `draw_range.py:469`), and no code assigns `max_electrical_power`. This group's consumer is the diagnostics value; routing the top into the solve is R10-COP-GATE's draw evidence (COP-GATE item 1), where R10-HEAT-METER's note assigns it. The test asserts that value, never a plan change.
- **Why not the plan path; the mild band.** Mapping through the plan's step would write the plan's level, not the experiment's, since `_step_kw` reads `power_schedule` (`pump_arbiter.py:462-466`). The ruling stands (mild-band top, 45-day lapse, configured p_max) with the 15 % rule as its only gate, its rationale corrected: the store learns *electrical* draw, and a cold inverter draws at least as much, so a mild-band top under-states the cold maximum — books less heat, never fictional heat.

## Alternatives rejected

1. **Every step at p_max, the bisection replaced by a duration search** (the review's option (i)): agrees the four by construction but runs the fit at the ceiling on every house (F1).
2. **Probe samples into the rolling window, `MIN_SAMPLES` lowered**: moves the clamp for every install, including the duty-cycling ones the brief holds unchanged, and a probe latches the clamp at its 48th append (F7).
3. **The converged guard lifted for the whole experiment**: lets one night overwrite a converged house's learned scale.
4. **The outcome at log level only**: #2808 asks for history and a timestamp; a log line is neither.
5. **Move the fold to before the solve, which is where its pair is still the interval's**: the fold's `defrost` argument is `_defrost_window.close(now)` (`coordinator.py:9829`), which must run on every cycle and would have to move or be called twice; carrying the pair costs one capture instead (re-cut A).
