## P88: record PRs batch 4-5 rows instead of one per push to main (S6-V23)
Verdict: ACCEPT -> a carry into R10-FLOW-RECORD-PITCH (the existing group that owns `record-autofix`'s open-and-automerge step and the record-chain carry from P49): on push events, open or update the record PR only once its plan holds at least 4 rowless merges or the oldest is 8 first-parent commits old; schedule and dispatch runs always flush.

Instances:
All counts are at the merge base `0810d5b8f`, over fixed UTC windows, so they do not slide as main moves. A record merge is a first-parent commit matching `git log --first-parent 0810d5b8f --since=<from> --until=<to> --grep='from tvofi/record/autofix'`.
- 2026-10-24T00:00Z to 2026-10-25T00:00Z: 19 record merges out of 46 first-parent merges (`--merges`), so 27 non-record merges. Null control: the same rule with the grep string `from tvofi/record/autofixZZ` returns 0. (Round 1 quoted 37 opened and 24 merged from a fuzzy `gh pr list --search 'record: delivery rows'`, which matches triage, fix and policy PRs too. That figure was wrong.)
- 2026-10-24T12:00Z to 2026-10-25T00:00Z: 9 record merges, rowing 2 to 10 PRs each. Over the whole day the 19 row 2 to 10 each, median 3. A row here is a file under `dev/programme/delivery/` that the merge adds or modifies against its first parent (`git diff --name-only --diff-filter=AM <c>^1 <c>`).
- All 19 of the day's record merges row the PR of the record merge before them (for example `e45c1546` rows #2754, which `a260e563` merged). Null control: the record PR four back is rowed by 0 of 19. Each record PR costs a gate run, a train `--bot-prs` sweep slot and a push to main. Every push to main moves main under every open head, and a train batch in flight answers that with a re-prove of about 30 to 45 min.
What the tree already does:
- `nightly.yml`'s `record-autofix` runs on every push to main, plans rows with `record_row.py --plan`, keeps one job-owned `record/autofix` branch (force-with-lease), and leaves an open record PR alone when `record_row.py --automerge-check --hold` passes, so it merges itself.
- `tests/delivery_status.py` calls a rowless merge OVERDUE only after `STALE_AFTER_COMMITS = 12` first-parent commits, a unit measured on batches (longest 8, median 4); anything under that is pending, not red.
- `stamp.py`'s rule 5 ("rows") refuses a tag while any merged PR in the window lacks a row (`delivery_status.py --require-rows`).
Cost: S inside R10-FLOW-RECORD-PITCH. About 10 to 20 lines in the `record-autofix` step: a count of the plan's rows (`record_row.py --row-numbers` already prints them), the first-parent age of the oldest, and `github.event_name`. Add an entities row and a harness case. No new file, no API calls, no Pi cost.
Risk, ruled:
- (a) Delivery-status lag: a row lands up to 8 first-parent commits after its merge. That is inside the 12-commit OVERDUE bound with a margin of 4, so the record never goes red from batching.
- (b) The stamp: rule 5 needs every row before the tag. The stamp step therefore dispatches `record-autofix` first and waits for its merge, which works because a dispatch run flushes whatever the count. That step goes in the carry.
- (c) HANDOVER trap 15 ("one record per merge has a bounded truth condition"): #531 failed because one record PR stayed open for 20 hours while 31 merges landed under it. Here the bound is explicit (8 commits), the PR is generated and never hand-edited, and once opened it still merges through the existing automerge guard. The rows stay one per merge; only the PR's timing batches. The orchestrator ruled it compatible on 2026-10-24: the 8-commit bound sits inside `STALE_AFTER_COMMITS`, and rows stay one per merge, so neither trap 15 nor the phrase "an immediate record PR" (`delivery-status-tracking.md`) changes.
- (d) The group is held behind R10-FLOW-MQ-PROBE (owner card 4, a carry on the group). The orchestrator decided on 2026-10-24 that there is no release during the drain (no new group starts). R10-FLOW-RECORD-PITCH is first in line after the drain, and its R10-FLOW-MQ-PROBE dependency is re-read then.
Binding: on 2026-10-24's traffic (19 record merges, 27 non-record merges), batching at 4 rowless merges gives about 9 record PRs a day while the record chain stands (each batch is 3 real merges plus the previous record merge, so 27 / 3) and about 7 once P49's chain fix lands (27 / 4, since a record merge then owes nothing). That removes some 10 to 12 gate runs, sweep slots and pushes to main a day, along with the re-proves those pushes cause. The 8-commit age arm only opens a PR earlier, so these are lower bounds on the PR count and upper bounds on the saving. It costs about 15 lines in a step the group already owns, against a lag bounded under the existing OVERDUE line. The verdict stays ACCEPT at about 10 to 12 saved a day.
