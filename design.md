## The defect, measured

`selfset_flat` in the harness runs on a 14 kW nameplate, where `MeasuredCop._base_folds` refuses a 1.5 kW ask under the 4.67 kW nameplate third, so it reads `0/96 1.000` and cannot show the defect. On a 4 kW nameplate (third 1.33 kW), with the same pump drawing 2.2 kW ±3 % whatever is asked under asks of 1.5 kW ±2 %, the 96-interval run ends at `92/96 0.500`, and so does a 1344-interval run (`COP_SCALE_MIN`). That holds at this base and at #3593's base 0a1ef512f4. Mechanism: the asks never span `FOLLOW_ASK_SPAN`, so `draw_range.ask_evidence` reads `ASK_UNOBTAINABLE` once `MIN_SAMPLES` run-asked pairs exist. `judge_ratio` and `release` then fold every departure by base's rule. With no heat reading, `flow_meter.cop_evidence` targets `scale × ask / draw`, and a draw the scale never moves compounds that target down to the clamp. Here the scale sits off 1.000 by more than 5 % for 625 of 672 cycles (the same run at this base).

**What separates the two pumps under flat asks.** In the harness's heat-led model (`true_<s>`: the meter reads ask × current scale / s), the draw answers the learner's own scale move. A self-setting pump's draw does not. So the evidence the slope test lacks when the asks are flat, the learner itself creates: each fold moves `cop_scale`, and `CopInterval` already stores the scale every row was read at. The regressor is the *heat ask* `asked_kw × cop_scale`. With flat asks, its span comes from the scale alone.

## Owning seam

One owner: `MeasuredCop` in `custom_components/heatpump_optimizer/accuracy.py`, the one place a departure is judged (`judge_ratio`, `release`). `draw_range.py` supplies the evidence state and its constants, unchanged. `coordinator.py` only calls: `_fold_measured_cop` (seam "learning" in `tests/seam_map.json`) applies the scale `MeasuredCop` hands back, through `_apply_cop_scale` ("learning"). `store.py` registers one stored key. No second owner.

## Shape

**Probe state on `MeasuredCop`** (no coordinator attribute): the probe rows, a deque bounded at `draw_range.WINDOW` of (heat ask, drawn kW); the origin, the scale the first probed row was read at; the verdict, one of pending, follows or ignored; and a pending restore.

**Admission, one private method, two callers.** A departure beyond `COP_ASK_TOLERANCE` that base's rule would fold under `ASK_UNOBTAINABLE` and **with a window** (`draw` not None) goes through it, whether it is judged live in `judge_ratio`'s last arm or released in `release`'s unobtainable and aged-out arms. With the verdict at follows, it folds. At ignored, it is refused. While pending, it joins the probe (the origin is set on the first row) and folds. Once the probe holds `draw_range.MIN_SAMPLES` rows whose heat asks span `FOLLOW_ASK_SPAN` (10th to 90th percentile, `LOW_PERCENTILE`/`HIGH_PERCENTILE`), the slope of log drawn on log heat ask decides by `FOLLOW_SLOPE_MIN`: at or above, the verdict latches follows; under it, ignored, and the restore is set to the origin. This adds no new constant: it is `ask_evidence`'s rule on a different regressor. `release` admits all of its unobtainable candidates first and judges once. On ignored it returns none of them, so a release never folds a row that the probe it fed refuses.

**Ignored is the window's does-not-follow, by another route.** While latched, `judge_ratio` returns `COP_REFUSED_OFF_ASK` for **every** interval, within the tolerance or not, as `ASK_IGNORED` does (#2262). That is an existing literal, so there are no card strings and no p1/p2 split (#3593 note n9).

**Restore, one coordinator site.** Right after the release loop in `_fold_measured_cop`, which runs past the freeze gate (departure 1, kept), one statement: if `MeasuredCop` hands back a restore, `_apply_cop_scale(origin)`. A verdict that `judge_ratio` latches is applied at the next unfrozen cycle's release site, one cycle late. A frozen cycle moves no scale, including this one. The probe lives only on installs with no heat reading (the `heat_kw` path skips `judge_ratio`; a counter returns `COP_REFUSED_SPAN_OPEN` first), where this fold is the only writer of `cop_scale` besides the load's `_apply_cop_scale` re-apply. So the restore undoes only this learner's own folds since the origin.

**Reset.** Every cycle `release` reads the state. Any state other than unobtainable clears the probe, the origin and the verdict, because the window's evidence (follows, does not follow) or a refilling window (pending, e.g. after `DrawRange.observe` dropped a reconfigured range) governs again.

**Persistence.** The latched verdict persists; the probe rows and the origin do not (like `held` and `ratio_ewma`). `MeasuredCop.as_dict` gains one optional key. `from_dict` reads it leniently: an unreadable or absent value is pending and **never drops the measurement record**, unlike the record's own fields. `store.py` registers `measured_cop/<key>` as a choice domain beside `measured_cop/cop`. Without the latch, each restart re-probes a self-setting pump and walks the scale out once more. A restart mid-probe leaves the scale where it walked, with no restore, so the fixer measures a reload row (Evidence).

**Diagnostics.** `accuracy.diagnostics_view` gains the verdict beside `draw_follows_ask`. Otherwise a `COP_REFUSED_OFF_ASK` refusal under `draw_follows_ask: None` explains nothing.

    _fold_measured_cop: freeze gate -> MeasuredCop.release(draw) [state != unobtainable: reset; unobtainable: admit, judge once]
                        -> fold returned rows (read scale) -> restore? _apply_cop_scale(origin)
                        -> ... judge_ratio(row) [latched ignored: COP_REFUSED_OFF_ASK; unobtainable departure: admit] -> fold

Invariants, each a test: (k) with a window whose evidence is follows, does-not-follow or pending, behaviour is bit-identical to this base. (l) With no window, base's rule stands and nothing is probed. (m) A heat-led pump under flat asks keeps its folds (latch follows). (n) A self-setting pump under flat asks ends at its origin scale, and every later interval reads `COP_REFUSED_OFF_ASK`. (o) The restore moves the scale only on an unfrozen cycle. (p) A verdict leaves only by a reset, and a reload keeps it. (q) An unreadable stored verdict keeps the measured COP.

## Alternatives, and why each lost

- **Hold under unobtainable too** (#3593's other arm): an install whose asks never move never folds a departure. That gives up invariant h (`held_full`, `held_filling`) for heat-led pumps, whose departures are the efficiency reading the learner exists for: a heat-led flat-ask pump of true scale 0.68 on 4 kW would stay at 1.000 where 0.68 is right.
- **Change `ask_evidence`'s regressor to the heat ask for every window**: the window would need a scale per sample (a `DrawRange` store column), and that second owner is `draw_range.py`. It would also re-judge spanning-ask installs whose draw follows the electrical ask (`p4_*` rows) on a regressor they do not follow.
- **Judge on the room's thermal response** (a self-setting pump over-delivers heat): this crosses into the heat-loss learners' seam, so it has two owners.
- **Bound every fold's move** (no verdict): it slows the walk to 0.500 and does not stop it.
- **Revert by replaying the inverse folds**: this needs per-row undo state. The origin is one float.
