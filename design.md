## Owning seams

- **Where a write came from** is decided once, in `pump_arbiter.py`: it alone knows whether this pass's water target was the open-loop map (`_flow_target`), the power-feedback loop's move (`_feedback`, inside or after `FEEDBACK_START_HOLD`), a boost, the gate, the fallback hold, or an experiment's level (`_experiment_kw`).
- **What a pass teaches about the map** (steady or not, kept or dropped) is `draw_range.py`'s, beside `fold` and the window; it takes no coordinator.
- **When an experiment runs and what it commands** is `sysid.py`'s (R10-CEILING-PROBE's statement).
- **What the debug week records and ships** is `debugger.py`'s; it only copies what the arbiter and the inputs already hold.
- `coordinator.py` only wires: one added line (the experiment's kind on its action), paid below. `thermal_model.py` and `diagnostics.py` do not change (the roster's `files` names both; this note is the authority, as the probe note was).

## A. Map evidence: what the store records

**Per arbitration pass** (cycle, one-minute tick, entity listener all enter `pump_arbiter.apply`), under Control only, the arbiter builds one pass record: time; written space set-point; gate, ceiling (`FlowCommand`), the map's own inputs (`flow_setpoint_for_level`'s level kW, band low and high from `_band_kw`, heat ceiling, the floor `modulation_floor_flow_c` returned, which carries the inlet, the gate and the lift `start_flow_min_lift_k` chose); the inlet as `_flow_inlet_c` read it; metered kW (`ArbiterInputs.measured_power_kw`); duty served; the mode select's reading; the defrost flag; and **origin**, one code: plan open-loop, feedback start-hold (open-loop), feedback-moved, boost, gate, hold, fit, probe, sweep. The record is kept as one field on `ArbiterState` (span 39) and handed to draw_range through `coord.accuracy.draw` (the coordinator's public view; no private reach).

**Segments, in draw_range.** Consecutive passes with the same written set-point, duty and origin form a segment; it closes on any change, a defrost pass, a compressor stop (metered kW at or under `RUNNING_FLOOR_KW`), or at 30 min (inlet drift). Its first 3 min are dropped (RESULT's hold rule; its residuals were stable at 2, 5 and 10 min). A closed segment with at least 2 steady minutes waits in an unpersisted buffer; the next `draw_range.fold` (already called once per cycle, `coordinator.py:10033` "experiment_s") commits it with that interval's `frozen` and `distorted` flags (dropped if either) and only for space-only duty. Row, 17 cells: start minute (epoch minutes), steady minutes, written, gate, ceiling, floor, level kW, band low, band high, heat ceiling, inlet mean, inlet change, outdoor, drawn mean, drawn spread, origin, duty (P7: 83 bytes).
**Retention**: a new module-level class in `draw_range.py`, one field on `DrawRange` holding a deque of 1008 rows (design constant: about four weeks at the 30-60 steady segments a day P1's install shows), 1008 x 83 = 84 KB in the accuracy store, saved at its existing cadence; not keyed to the configured range (a row carries its band), so a reconfiguration keeps it. `DrawRange.summary` adds the row count; the rows ride `as_dict` to the store and so to the bundle's `stores` section.
**Routing invariant.** A sweep interval (any pass of the closed interval with origin sweep, read from the buffer) never reaches `DrawRange.observe`: at the ceiling it takes `observe_ceiling` exactly as a probe interval, below it nothing but map rows. So no sweep draw can move the window, the latch, `_run_asked`, `ask_evidence` or `power_floor_kw`; the approved draw-floor judgement sees what it sees today. The fit's and probe's routing is unchanged.
**Migration.** Additive key in the accuracy store's `draw` document; a stored record without it reads an empty deque in `DrawRange.from_dict`, a malformed row is dropped alone (the `_stored_ceiling` pattern); `ACCURACY_STORE_VERSION` stays; `store.DOMAINS` gains the 17 row leaves, or D15's store replay reports them off-domain (round 10 s1-05 found exactly that class).
**Diagnostics.** No new redaction surface: numbers and fixed codes, no entity id, name or coordinate; the existing keyed and value-search passes run over the whole payload. A coordinate-valued planted reading in the synthetic fixture is the privacy null (F).

## B. The sweep: a third experiment kind

**Kind** `KIND_SWEEP` beside `KIND_FIT` and `KIND_PROBE`. `armable` never picks it: it is armed only by its own button press ("Measure the pump's power response"), which calls the existing arm path with the kind requested. Eligibility is the probe's (`thermal_model.ceiling_probe_install`: meter and flow set-point driven), else a published refusal; the option "Allow a one-off measurement experiment" must be on (no new options key).
**Schedule.** The band is read from the plant at arm (`arm_from_config` already passes it). After the existing 1 h settle (the plan runs; baseline room temperature), five levels, each held one cycle (`DEFAULT_OPTIMIZATION_INTERVAL`, 30 min), band fractions 0.5, 1.0, 0.25, 0.75, 0.0: steps of both signs and two sizes, so inlet drift is not confounded with level, the top level last-but-one and the floor last, where a controller stop hands back to the plan. Level kW = low + fraction x (high - low); the arbiter maps it as it maps any experiment (`_experiment_kw`, `_band_kw` on the hub's band) and `_feedback_on` is already False for an experiment's action: every hold is open-loop by construction, and its segments carry origin sweep. A module function in `sysid.py` returns the level for an elapsed time; the class gains only the branches in `_begin_step_phase`, `step`, `_finish`.
**Guards.** Comfort: before the first level, `_predict_step_excursion_plant` at the schedule's mean level x COP over 2.5 h must stay inside `max_excursion_c`, else abort "no sweep fits within the comfort bound"; the live over-excursion abort stands. `hold_reason` (learner freeze, a mode that cannot heat, a quiet window) stands a started sweep down (abort). Hot water keeps priority: the action keeps the plan's hot-water power (as the probe's does), the arbiter serves a both step by its split, and that level simply holds fewer space-only minutes; no retry (a retry needs the store inside the experiment, which the off-loop step copies, `step_detached`). Defrost minutes close segments. Pump limits: levels stay in the configured band and set-points inside the entity range and controller limits (`_bounded`, `_setpoint_limits_c`). Compressor starts: no level asks idle, so a sweep adds at most the one start of a pump idle at its start.
**Price.** Same gates as the fit: night window, outdoor band, and price at or under the horizon's 30th percentile, so on a spiky day it defers to a cheap night hour and never runs into a peak. Unlike the probe it does not refuse a flat price (the measurement is not price-motivated, and a flat-tariff install would otherwise never get one). Cost, electrical energy commanded: 0.5 h x the five levels = 2.5 x low + 1.25 x (high - low) kWh; on P1's band (0.5 to 4.0 kW, RESULT section 1) 5.625 kWh, an upper bound on extra cost of 5.625 x the flat price (the plan's own heat in those 2.5 h is displaced and the next solve draws down what the house stored; the body claims only the bound). Flat-price null: the cost figure is that product and nothing else.
**Ends.** Completion or abort leaves: committed map rows marked sweep, the top level's intervals in the ceiling store (a side benefit for part 1's learned top), a result reason "power-response sweep held N of 5 levels" in the existing recorded outcome attribute, nothing adopted, the plan from the next cycle. Runtime 3.5 h, inside the 23-05 window. No clock: one press, one run.
**Wiring.** The arbiter must tell a sweep's action from a fit's: the coordinator's override dict gains the experiment kind (one line beside the `"mode": "system_identification"` at its adopt site; `CurrentAction` gains the key, type only in `payload.py`).

## E. Alternatives, and the smallest conclusive version

- **Widen the window rows with inlet and time.** Lost: the window pairs the set-point held at an interval's start with the meter at its close (RESULT section 1), which a timestamp does not repair, and its rows feed the latch and the draw-floor judgement, so a sweep row there would move them.
- **Hold a fixed set-point instead of a fixed level.** Lost on call path: it needs a second actuation branch in `_flow_target`; holding the level measures exactly the write the map produces, and the row keeps the set-point and inlet, so either fit runs.
- **Sweep as a plan override in the optimizer.** Lost: a second mechanism beside sysid, with its own comfort and price gates.
- **A second recorder for the trace** (owner: one mechanism). Not designed.
- **Per-day trace files.** Lost on store keys: each needs a `store.DOMAINS` table, a factory-reset entry and a `store_keys` entry; the ring's hourly rewrite costs at most 168 rewrites of under 2 MB a collection week, opt-in.
- **Deduplicate repeated accuracy samples in cycle rows.** Refused on P5: 36 KB a week.
- **Smallest conclusive version**: B plus A's rows, one sweep night on each of two inlet levels: ten open-loop steady points at five band fractions, each with inlet and time, which measures the level and the gain the closed-loop store could not; curvature below RESULT's detector bound (about 1 kW) stays unclaimed.

## F. A D15-complete debug week

Derived from D15's method steps and round 10's unfinished cells (`dev/audit/rounds/round10/D15/` s1 to s3: M5 and M8 unfinished on a null replay half, self-tests null mid-collection) and this month's gaps (P1: a 24 h replay of a 7-day collection; P3: why a week cannot fit).
1. **Minute trace** (M5, M8, M7; the map): while a collection is active the collector arms its own one-minute timer and appends one cell per column: the arbiter's pass record (A) and each mapped input's state (`replay_doc.mapped_entities`, person and calendar already excluded by `debugger._public`), columns keyed by config key, never entity id; numeric cells rounded to the entity's precision, text cells only for select, switch and binary-sensor inputs. Encoding P4's: one string per column per day, empty = unchanged, a gap column for minutes Home Assistant was down. Size: about 0.45 MB a week of inputs (P4) plus an estimated 0.2 MB of arbiter columns; held in the existing ring (7 days, deleted when the option is turned off); the closed days encoded, the open day as float arrays (1440 x about 40 cells). Fit: P2's 3,663,331 + about 660,000 + the 24 h replay 3,375,297 + `DOWNLOAD_HEADROOM_BYTES` stays under the cap, so the replay window does not shrink on P1's install.
2. **Lifecycle rows** (M1): one row per collector load and close with time and release, so restart gaps are read, not inferred by `d15_restart_gaps.py`.
3. **Per-cycle learner freeze and input verdict** (M7, the round-10 note that only the newest snapshot carried the unhealthy input): `cycle_row` gains the payload's learner-freeze reason and input-health verdict.
4. **Version marker**: `BUNDLE_SCHEMA` becomes hpo-debug/2 and the manifest gains a section map (name to version) and the trace's first minute, minutes and missing minutes. `tools/replay/debug_ingest.py` accepts both; on a hpo-debug/1 bundle every trace check reports unfinished, never pass (round 10 s1-02's vacuous pass on an absent replay). `tools/replay/dbg_bundle_gen.py` emits hpo-debug/2 with a planted trace and keeps a version 1 arm.
5. **Privacy**: nothing the redaction removes can reappear: no entity id or name is a trace key, the bundle stays inside the payload both diagnostics passes walk, and `tests/replay.py`'s leak probes gain a planted coordinate and a planted person id as trace cells (null: the clean trace passes `export.violations`).
**A D15 seat could then**: replay the integration's inputs and writes for the whole week at minute resolution, reproduce an anomaly window outside the replay half, compare plan, write and pump response per minute (feedback behaviour, interruptions, map error), read restarts directly, and fit the set-point-to-draw map. **Still unanswerable**: sub-minute dynamics outside the replay window; pump internals no entity exposes (valve position, controller integrator); unmapped entities; published-entity history outside the replay window (cycle rows and daily snapshots only); anything before the collection started.
