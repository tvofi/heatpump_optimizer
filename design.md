## A. Map evidence

**Pass record.** Per arbitration pass under Control the arbiter records: time; written space set-point; gate and ceiling (`FlowCommand`); the map's inputs (level kW, band low and high from `_band_kw`, heat ceiling, the floor from `modulation_floor_flow_c`); the inlet as `_flow_inlet_c` read it; metered kW (`ArbiterInputs.measured_power_kw`); duty served; the mode select's reading; the defrost flag; and **origin**: plan open-loop, feedback start-hold, feedback-moved, boost, gate, hold, fit, probe, sweep. One field on `ArbiterState`; handed to draw_range through `coord.accuracy.draw` (the coordinator's public view).
**Segments.** Consecutive passes with the same written set-point, duty and origin; closed on any change, a defrost pass, a compressor stop (kW at or under `RUNNING_FLOOR_KW`), a sweep level change, or 30 min; first 3 min dropped; at least 2 steady minutes. Because a sweep level's set-point is held fixed (B), a sweep level is one segment up to its 30 min (P9's start arm); the rule is not changed for plan passes, whose fragmentation under feedback is the evidence that they are closed-loop. The next `draw_range.fold` commits closed segments, dropped where its `frozen` or `distorted` flag is set; space-only duty only. Row: start minute, steady minutes, written, gate, ceiling, floor, level kW, band low, band high, heat ceiling, inlet mean, inlet change, outdoor, drawn mean, drawn spread, origin, duty.
**Storage [R4].** Kept and persisted as 17 column strings (one per field, comma-separated), never as row lists: P7 prices 1008 rows at 79,006 bytes columnar against 444,478 as lists. Cap 672 rows (design constant), about 52.7 KB (P7 scaled), not keyed to the configured range. Write volume (r7): the accuracy store is saved every cycle (`_async_save_accuracy` in the cycle's step list), 48 times a day, so the rows add about 2.5 MB a day of writes, always on; the fixer measures the store's bytes before and after on the synthetic week.
**Routing invariant (one place, `draw_range.fold`).** A sweep interval (any pass of the closed interval with origin sweep) never reaches `DrawRange.observe` and never an idle reading; at the band top it reaches the ceiling store [CE]; below it only map rows. The fit's and probe's routing is unchanged.
**Migration.** Additive key; a record without it reads empty in `DrawRange.from_dict` through a module reader beside `_stored_ceiling` (r4: `from_dict` is at cc 15); `ACCURACY_STORE_VERSION` stays; `store.DOMAINS` gains the column leaves.

## B. The sweep

**Kind and arming [CE, r3].** `KIND_SWEEP`, armed only by its own button ("Measure the pump's power response"), passing the requested kind through `async_arm_system_identification` and `arm_from_config` into `arm` and `armable`; `armable` never picks it unasked. In `arm` the sweep takes the probe's branch (no slab identifiability gate) and `_finish` never sets the fit's `last_run`. Eligibility is `thermal_model.ceiling_probe_install`; the experiment option must be on.
**Schedule and the held set-point [R6].** After the existing 1 h settle, up to five levels of one cycle (30 min) each, order 0.0, 0.5, 0.25, 1.0, 0.75 (low first: P9's start arm keeps the low levels below the rising inlet). At the sweep's first step pass the arbiter maps all five set-points once, on the inlet then (`flow_setpoint_for_level` on that floor), and holds each for its level: one new branch in the arbiter's experiment path, keyed on the action's kind, storing the five on `ArbiterState`. Start condition: the band at start, ceiling minus floor, at least 8 K (P9: at a 42 °C inlet the five span 3 K and collapse), else the arm stays armed with the held reason "the water is too warm for a sweep". `_feedback_on` is already False for an experiment's action, so every hold is open-loop.
**Levels per night [R1].** Before the first level, sysid predicts the room along the k-level schedule plus `relax_hours`, through the loop `_predict_step_excursion_plant` already runs (generalised to a list of (heat, hours) pairs), twice: every level at the band top times COP (the draw the install can reach; RESULT measured +1.7 to +2.3 kW over the command at the floor end) and every level at its commanded kW; both must stay within `max_excursion_c`. k is the largest of 5 to 2 that fits (P8); under 2 the arm refuses "no sweep fits within the comfort bound". The k levels are those with the fewest sweep rows in A's store, read at arm (on the loop, not in the off-loop step), so successive presses complete the set. The live `_over_excursion` abort stays the backstop.
**Hot water [R2].** Any experiment's step phase is space or idle duty (`_planned_duty`, the experiment branch: "a hot-water charge would share the meter"), so hot water is suspended for the sweep's k x 30 min, up to 2.5 h; a held disinfection is the exception (`_disinfecting`). Bound, sweep only: when the tank reads below the pump's own hot-water start temperature (`_dhw_start_below_c`), the arbiter serves hot water for that pass (origin sweep, duty hot water, so no map row) and the level's steady minutes shrink, visible in the yield report. The fit and probe keep today's suspension (stated, not changed).
**Price and hour [R5b].** Start: the fit's gates (night window, outdoor band, price at or under the horizon's 30th percentile), plus the remaining night window at least the settle and k levels, and every horizon price inside the run's span at or under the cutoff. At each level boundary the hour and current price are re-checked; failing either finishes the sweep early with the levels held so far (rows kept, nothing aborted). A flat horizon passes (cutoff equals the price).
**Cost [R5a].** Bound: k x 0.5 h x the band top: 10 kWh for five levels on P1's 4.0 kW top (RESULT section 1). The run publishes its metered energy, the integral of the pass records' kW over its step phase, in the yield report. Null: on a synthetic flat-price run the published figure equals the meter's integral over the run.
**Yield report [R6].** sysid's step runs off the loop on a copy (`step_detached`) and cannot read the store, so the yield is computed in draw_range from the sweep's rows and published in `draw_range.diagnostics_view` as the last sweep: per level the fraction, held set-point, steady minutes, drawn mean, inlet mean, plus metered kWh; sysid's outcome reason says "power-response sweep held N of k levels; per-level yield in the draw diagnostics". A collapsed sweep shows as levels with zero steady minutes, not as points.
**Ends.** Completion, early finish or abort leaves map rows marked sweep, the band-top interval in the ceiling store [CE], nothing adopted, the plan from the next cycle. A reload mid-sweep ends it with no outcome reason, an armed press is lost and A's segment buffer drops (`SystemIdentification` is rebuilt at setup), r1. Starts: no more than a plan step at the same level (r2), counted in the synthetic run.
**Wiring.** The arbiter and the COP judgement need the kind: the override dict gains it (one line at its adopt site); `CurrentAction` gains the key (type only, `payload.py`); the pending capture reads it beside `mode`.

### [CE] Ceiling evidence on an unconverged install

Two changes, one decision place each, both deliberate:
1. **The sweep's band-top level feeds the learned top**, through the probe's own gate and evidence rule (`DrawRange.takes_ceiling`: written within `CEILING_TOLERANCE_K` of the ceiling, drawn above the idle margin, an experiment's interval, running space, not frozen, distorted or defrosting) and `observe_ceiling`. A band-top sweep hold is the probe's command exactly, open-loop. Which stores a sweep interval may feed, decided in `draw_range.fold`: map rows yes, ceiling store yes (band-top level only), window no, idle no; and in `MeasuredCop` [R3]: COP scale, COP health and capacity envelope no. Each ceiling row gains a third cell, its source (probe or sweep; a stored two-cell row reads probe), so the probe's clock in `armable` reads probe rows only and a sweep sample never postpones a probe (r5); the learned top's median reads both.
2. **The button arms the probe first where the top is unlearned.** In `armable`: an install that can probe, whose store has no learned top and whose probe clock allows, arms the probe even while the house learner is unconverged; otherwise the fit, then the probe, as today. The learned top changes every plan's booked electrical level (RESULT: -0.79 kW at the ceiling, `solve_band` reason "awaiting_probe"); the fit only refines the house. Three probe runs at the 7-day unlearned clock learn the top; the fit resumes after. Decision: owner (below).
**What the button says.** The arm's published result names the kind armed and why ("armed the ceiling probe: the pump's top is not learned yet" or "armed the fit; the ceiling probe waits N days"), in the existing recorded outcome attribute.
**Null controls.** An install that cannot probe arms exactly as base (byte-identical result); a probe-capable install with a learned top arms as base; only unlearned, capable and clock-allowed moves. A sweep run's ceiling rows are source sweep and leave `armable`'s probe clock unchanged; the same run without its band-top level leaves the ceiling store unchanged.

## E. Alternatives, and the smallest conclusive version

- **Hold the level and re-map every pass** (round 1): lost on P9: with any inlet drift a level writes up to 21 set-points and later levels collapse to the ceiling.
- **Map each level's set-point at that level's start**: lost on P9: the floor rises with the water, so by the fourth level the band has collapsed at 0.2 K/min.
- **Widen the window rows with inlet and time**: lost: the window pairs a start set-point with a close meter reading, and feeds the latch and the draw-floor judgement.
- **Learner exclusion in `_cop_fold_blocked` or the coordinator**: lost: a second home for the efficiency judgement, and `_fold_measured_cop` sits at cc 15.
- **Sweep samples count toward the probe's clock**: lost (r5): one sweep sample would hold off a four-sample probe for 7 days.
- **Arm the probe automatically**: refused: experiments run only on an owner's press.
- **Per-day trace files; deduplicated accuracy samples (P5, 36 KB)**: refused as in round 1.
- **Smallest conclusive version**: B's held set-points with A's rows, two sweep nights at inlets under 37 °C: up to ten open-loop steady points across the band (P9's start arm), each with inlet and time, which fixes level and gain; curvature under RESULT's detector bound (about 1 kW) stays unclaimed.
