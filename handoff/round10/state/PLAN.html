How the ETAs are computed

Every figure on this page is plan.json's, written by tools/plan/replan.py; this renderer draws it and computes no schedule of its own. A wave is the set of groups dispatched at one moment, and the next opens as soon as one of 20 pull-request slots frees, so a wave never waits for the previous one to drain; the cap limits concurrency, not the wave boundary. Duration is dispatch to merge by roster size, as the plan recorded it from tools/plan/durations.json: S 21.6 h, M 32.4 h, L 35.6 h, XL 35.6 h. p75 over 348 done groups with a merged PR (S n=227, M n=94, L n=21, XL n=6): active hours (the union of each part's first commit or creation, or the roster's dispatch stamp, to its merge) with extra review rounds priced out at the pooled Theil-Sen slope (5.65 h/round), then the cell's mean rounds added back; per class where a cell has n>=5. Wall clock: review, queueing and the PR-less handoff wait are inside it. carry_hours (0.28 h) is the Theil-Sen slope of the round-adjusted hours on the group's carry count, priced per carry above the size's median (carry_baseline). Monotone in size: XL 29.9 h lifted to L's 35.6 h. Measured by tools/plan/size_durations.py; re-derive, never edit. Input: 1021 merged PRs created since 2026-10-12, read by size_durations.fetch_rest (--via rest). A group whose fixer is already working gets 70% of its size duration as the remaining estimate, and starts now, past the slot cap, unless an unmerged dependency holds it; a held (carries-open) group is priced by its open carries, not its size. Hover an ETA for the group's tier, why it starts when it does, its duration basis and its sort key, all the plan's own fields. Those are measured lead times, not effort: review rounds and queueing are inside them. The batching window is 0 minutes; its measured trade is in durations.json.
