A fix reviewer ran the check by hand; no job went red on the PR.

- **Process state (c):** two processes were followed and neither could see it. `graders-head-copy` in `tests.yml` (decision 0013's amendment) is keyed on the grader itself changing and covers only `coverage_ratchet` and `delivery_status`; `tools/audit/prepr.sh` runs head copies of `policy_lint`, `rules_sync`, `fragments_sync`, the hooks and the wave script, but never `codeowners_gap`. The pin (`51b0742`) predates the checker (`b6e3029`), so not (d).
- **Not the #1589 class:** #1589's head was green on its own tree; it merged after #1592 added quoted paths to `policy_lint.mjs` without re-grading, a stale-base semantic conflict.
- **Class reach:** 9 programs run in pinned jobs that also grade `main`; before the countermeasure `codeowners_gap` and `brief_lint` had no local path. Over 76 first-parent `main` commits from `b6e3029^`, `codeowners_gap` was red on 8 (5 h 24 min, 7 merges landing on red, repaired by #1604).
- **Cost test:** `codeowners_gap` adds about 31–88 s per release and `brief_lint` 4.1 s on branches touching a pinned path, against P ≈ 0.2–0.6 per release × an expected ≥ 6–18 min per instance (the escaped instance cost 5.4 h). Passes.
