Round-9 F10.1b: the Home Assistant stub's clock is aware in UTC by default, and its Store honours `version`. Class P11 (RCA seat P11, `/mnt/project-files/audit-r9/rca/p11/RCA.md`; the barrier landed in F10.1).

Fixes #1649 (P11). Part of #1740. Part of #201.

Before: `dt_util.now()` in the stub returned a naive datetime when no zone was configured, where Home Assistant's is aware in UTC, so the naive-versus-aware verdict of every stored-stamp comparison was inverted (6 of 6 cells, D1-s1-52). `DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE` was `None`, where upstream's is UTC and never `None`, so `stored_instant(raw, dt_util.DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE)` left a typed time naive. `Store` took `version` and discarded it, so a version bump with no migration loaded fine (#1740).

After: `now()` is aware in `DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE`, which is UTC unless `HASTUB_TZ` names another zone; a naive freeze is read as UTC wall time. A load at another major version raises `NotImplementedError` unless the Store defines `_async_migrate_func`, which is then called with the stored version, as upstream's `_async_load_data` does. `as_local` stays the identity under the UTC default, so no golden fixture moves. `util.dt.now` and `util.dt.DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE` are FAITHFUL in `tests/ha_contract.py` and their contracts are `expect="both"`; a stub-only `Store` version contract is new.

How: 0 production lines. Stub edits in `tests/hastub/homeassistant/util/dt.py` and `helpers/storage.py`; contracts in `tests/ha_contract.py`; fixtures and a class-named `R9 P11` block in `tests/features.py`; one carry entry in `.claude/workflows/carry-1649.json`. The brief's carry said D1-s3-01's away check would pass once F3.1 was on the merge base. It does not, on its own: `away._parse_return_time` reads a typed time through `stored_instant(raw, dt_util.DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE)`, and with the stub's zone `None` the value stays naive. Making the stub's zone UTC, as upstream's, is the fix, and is why `DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE` is in this diff.
