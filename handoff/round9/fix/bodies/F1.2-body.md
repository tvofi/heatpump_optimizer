Why this change, and what it measures.

Three round-9 findings in the coordinator lane, measured at merge base
`2d71759e` (origin/main with F1.1 merged) and at the code head below:

- **D12-s1-01** (P6, high): with the tank probe omitted (the config flow's
  default install) every solve started from the 55 °C `ThermalState` default,
  never advanced; the slab from a one-time room+1 K seed. `_update_current_state`
  now seeds both from the previous plan's own trajectory at the step covering
  now (module-level `_open_loop_plan_value`; the `get_current_action` step
  rule, instants not wall clocks). A probe reading still wins.
- **D12-s3-01** (P2, medium; merged D4-s2-04 and D12-s1-02): the full wizard's
  `dhw` and `zones` pages offered only defaulted optional fields, so an
  untouched submit stored tank volume and zone keys whose presence alone
  configured the plant. Both pages now ask presence (`dhw_enabled`; a zone
  question writing `two_zone_mode`) with the stored answer suggested back,
  the way `quick_setup.derive` writes the explicit flags. The merged
  finding's options half (D12-s1-02): an untouched Hot water / Hot water
  tank options page over a no-hot-water entry stored the presence-inferred
  pair; `_omit_unstored_defaults` now drops that pair at its own form
  defaults over an entry with no hot water at all (a stored key and an
  edited value keep today's behaviour).
- **D14-s2-01** (P2, medium): the two-zone and wood-furnace facts were
  re-derived from a proxy key at three seams beside their canonical
  predicate. `_derive_preset` and `modbus_prefill._plant` take the two-zone
  verdict from `ThermalParameters.from_config(...).two_zone_enabled`;
  `topology.describe_setup`'s wood picture from `wood_furnace_on`
  (`_wood_tank_shown` deleted).

Borrows `config_flow.py`, `modbus_prefill.py` and `topology.py` from F5: F5.2
does not open until this merges.
