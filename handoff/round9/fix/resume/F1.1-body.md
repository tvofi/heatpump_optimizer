Before: Home Assistant gives every aware stamp one shared ZoneInfo object, and CPython subtracts two such stamps as wall clock. `+ timedelta` on them adds wall time as well. So across a DST transition each of these was an hour off:
- `_forecast_arrays`' grid offset (42 of 48 cycles priced a quarter from the wrong hour);
- `_record_accuracy`'s elapsed time;
- both lead-prediction filers;
- `_next_optimization`;
- `_detect_outage`'s gap and window;
- `accuracy.score_lead_predictions`;
- `external_heat._rate`;
- `dhw_learning.async_learn_dynamics`.

`tests/replay.py` stepped its zoned clock in wall time too, which ran 12 cycles on spring day and 8 on autumn day where 10 were due. The `_detect_outage` and `_immersion_dhw_margin` tzinfo guards and Open-Meteo's naive-is-UTC line were each deletable with the suite green, because the suite's clock was naive and its process zone UTC. Under an away setback the climate entity published the solve's 16.0 °C setback as its target for the length of a solve.

After: every one of those seams measures or shifts in UTC through two pure helpers in `accuracy.py`, `utc_elapsed_seconds` and `utc_shift`. The replay lane walks `run_fixture` and its price and forecast builders in UTC. The DST tracer fails any production `-` or `+` whose zoned answer differs from UTC truth on replayed spring and autumn days. Each guard is pinned under an aware clock or a non-UTC process zone. `target_temperature` reads the user's configured target from the entry.

How: `accuracy.py` gains `_as_utc`, `utc_elapsed_seconds` and `utc_shift` as module-level helpers. `coordinator.py`, `external_heat.py` and `dhw_learning.py` call them at each seam. The tracer wraps `dt_util`'s `now`, `utcnow`, `as_local`, `as_utc` and `parse_datetime` in a `datetime` subclass that judges each operation against UTC truth, keyed by file and function. It drives `replay.run_fixture` over the committed replay day shifted onto 2026-03-29, 2026-10-25 and a plain 2026-10-18. `tariff._window_slot` is exempt: its wall-clock step is its contract, and the plain-day null shows the tracer reaches it.
