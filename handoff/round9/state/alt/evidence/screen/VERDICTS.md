## Details the table cannot hold

- **S1 mechanism:** `boost.apply` → `overlay()` mutates `coord._current_action` in place. That dict is the same object as `data["current_action"]`.
  - Nothing removes the overlay keys when the channel ends.
  - `_current_action` is only replaced on a successful solve or in the fixed modes.
  - `no_prices` and `solve_failed` return before the replacement.
  - `_apply_action` re-sends the stale action while `_plan_is_stale()` is False: 3 × 30 min, floor 90 min.
  - The fixed modes (comfort/boost/off) rebuild the dict every cycle and are immune.
- **S2 window width** is one shadow solve. Arm (a) needs no slow hardware. Arm (b) needs the borrowed solve to exceed `SIMULATE_MIN_INTERVAL_SECONDS` = 3 s. That was emulated by holding the gate 3.2 s, which is realistic for Pi-class solves.
- **H1 persistence:** the snapshot `data_B` stays as `coordinator.data` until the next refresh. `data_C` (built after the window, away still active) publishes the configured values. So the published band flips with timing, not with state.
- **H3 R5:** refuted statically. `DhwProfileLearner` is constructed once in `__init__` (coordinator ~2152), before any solve, so the ~96 seed copies the configured pattern.
- **C1 premise:** the probe drives the unpatched `_await_optimize`. Only `_await_process` (transport) and `optimize_in_process` are recorders. `ProcessWorkerUnavailable` is raised for pickling or transport errors, which can be job-specific. Whether any shipped configuration makes one entry's job unpicklable was not established.
- **C2 adjacent finding (not in the lead):** `async_apply_manual_plan`, and any `async_request_refresh`, issued while a solve is in flight is absorbed by the `_optimization_running` early return. The new request is not solved until the next scheduled interval, and the service response reports its pinned steps as if applied.
