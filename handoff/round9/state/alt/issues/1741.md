## What

**1. The step-start clock is defined twice.**
- `_utc_step_starts` exists at `coordinator.py:553` as `(midnight, n_steps, step_offset, step_minutes)` and at `optimizer.py:199` as `(start, n, dt_hours)`: one UTC-walking rule, two signatures.
- `silent_mode.py:58` imports the optimizer copy; `tests/dst_checks.py` and `tests/features.py` import both.
- **Measured** (`m6a_step_starts.out`): the two agree on 18 of 18 reachable grids, which covers both DST days at steps of 5 to 60 minutes. That is not a behaviour defect today.
  - The rounding in the `_horizon_step_starts` bridge (`coordinator.py:7508-7513`) is unreachable, because `dt_hours` is `time_step_minutes / 60` (`optimizer.py:1343`).
- **Control:** a step that is not a whole number of minutes does disagree. So the agreement is a property of today's config surface that no single definition holds; the only pin is `tests/dst_checks.py:421-425`, spring day, dt = 0.25.

**2. The tank-room ambient is a literal `20.0` at eight sites.** Every one is "tank or buffer loss to a ~20 °C indoor ambient":
- `thermal_model.py:2225`, `2312` (`_simulate_step_two_zone`) and `2946`, `2983` (`simulate_trajectory_batch`, its batch twin)
- `sysid.py:201`
- `optimizer.py:4796`, `6570`, `6934`

A change at one site would silently disagree across the plant, the batch twin, system identification and the planner.

## Fix shape

- One step-start function taking a timedelta step and an offset.
- One named value for the tank-room ambient.
- Pure; goldens byte-identical.
- Extend the DST agreement pin to the autumn day.
