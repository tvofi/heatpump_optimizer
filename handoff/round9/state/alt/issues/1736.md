## What

None of the three classes is frozen (`ThermalParameters` 81 fields, `OptimizationConfig` 33, `ThermalState` 15, plain `@dataclass`). Coordinator-wide: 65 in-place write sites over 50 hub fields.

One mutable object therefore carries two meanings, the configured value and this solve's effective value, and every reader has to know which one it holds at the instant it reads.

## Fix shape (R9-EG-B1)

- The configured objects stop being written per solve.
- Each solve builds one per-solve input record, consumed by the live solve, the fuse advisor, the price tiles and the what-if.
- `optimize` takes that record keyword-only instead of its 14 positional arrays. Its own comment (`optimizer.py:2805`) concedes that a positional transposition would be silent.
- The setback becomes a value in the record, so the compare-and-restore envelope in `away.py` is deleted, not extended.

It is a pure refactor: goldens stay byte-identical. Any what-if parity gap the root-cause seat finds goes to the owner as a separate product decision, not a rider on this change.
