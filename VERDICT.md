## Revise points (defects in the design)

1. **shape — the ceiling write and the fit's sized step disagree; the cadence's arm path is unreachable.** `pump_arbiter.py:744-746` and the proposed `_flow_target` branch key on `(inp.action or {}).get("mode")`, and `coordinator.py:10985-10991` books that mode for *both* kinds. The fit's override is `self._step_power`, set from `_size_step_power` at `sysid.py:1547`/`1564` and returned by `step()`; the sizer searches the *power* down so the excursion fits. As written, a fit whose sized step is below p_max is commanded the configured ceiling while its override, its booked `power` and the power its excursion bound was computed at are the sized step — so the comfort bound the note adds does not bound the fit's commanded step. The note's "runs the existing sizer/rollout … with the step fixed at configured p_max x COP" is not a call `_size_step_power` supports (its first argument is a bound it searches under). Fix wanted: state the sized variable per kind — either (i) every system-identification step is `_step_power = configured p_max`, the power bisection replaced by a duration search at p_max, `_unadoptable` taking `p_max × COP`, and override/booking at p_max so action power, command and excursion agree; or (ii) the ceiling branch gated on the action's power at p_max, the fit keeping its sized step, and the note justifying that mapping against the comfort bound. Separately, `arm()` still reads only the fit's `last_run` (`sysid.py:1365-1374`), so on a house whose fit ran inside 30 days `arm()` refuses before a probe is chosen and the note's "else the probe" cannot happen; the kind must be selected at arm and `arm(kind)` must check that kind's own clock.

2. **class-open — the note's own grep returns #2808's two surfaces, and both are unchanged.** `button.py:147-152` stays available whenever the experiment is not active; `sensor.py:1995-1997` keeps the whole `system_identification` view — result reason and `last_run` included (`sysid.py:2345`) — in `_unrecorded_attributes`. The note's sibling list omits both although its own command returns them. So the design's "outcome published as the result reason" writes into an unrecorded attribute: no history, no logbook entry, no timestamp — #2808's third complaint verbatim. On a converged house that is not probe-eligible (no meter, or `flow_command` `None`), `arm()` still succeeds and `conditions_met` still refuses with "house already converged" (`sysid.py:1401`, `1649-1652`), silently — #2808's first complaint. And the freeze still aborts an armed experiment (`coordinator.py:10958-10963`) — #2808's second — which the note does not mention. Fix wanted: for each of the three, fix it at the named site or refuse it with a reason: refuse at `arm()` (or make the button unavailable) with a published reason for a house the probe cannot serve; record the outcome (move it, or a timestamped field, out of `_unrecorded_attributes` at `sensor.py:1995-1997`, or add a logbook/repairs entry or a visible log level); and say why the abort is kept rather than a hold, with the abort visible. Add `button.py` and `sensor.py` to "Sibling sites".

3. **shape — the learned top has no production consumer, and (a) rests on a clamp that is not wired.** `draw_range.planned_range` (`draw_range.py:438`) is called only by `diagnostics_view` (`:469`); nothing in the solve reads `DrawRange.planned`/`.engaged`, and no code writes a metered value into `params.max_electrical_power` (checked across `custom_components`). So (a)'s "duty `space` follows `_band_kw`'s p_max and self-censors once the top is learned" and the mild-band line "the planner clamps to min(learned, configured)" describe a live clamp that does not exist; the probe's top is a diagnostics number until the deferred wiring lands (R10-SETPOINT-POWER's "do not wire `draw_range.planned_range` into the solve until the mapping is fixed", still unwired at main). Fix wanted: name the consumer and the file that would change, or state that the top is learning-only with the deferral named and the wiring out of this group; correct the self-censoring sentence to the real reason for the branch (`_step_kw` reads the plan's `power_schedule`, not the experiment's override, so a mapping would follow the plan); and make the test assert `DrawRange.observed()`/`planned()` or `diagnostics_view`'s `effective_max_kw`, not a plan change.

4. **evidence — the probe-window figure is contradicted by the finder's own second line.** The note says "observed() gives (1.5, None) and the clamp stays unengaged even at 48 (48/336 below `DISAGREE_SHARE`). So probe samples do not enter the rolling window's percentile". `probe_draw.py`'s `run(48)` prints `((1.5, 3.0), False, None)`: at 48 full ceiling samples `observed()`'s high *is* the probe samples, because `full.size >= MIN_SAMPLES` (`draw_range.py:203-213`). What is true is narrower: 48 samples do not engage the latch on a full 336-sample window (48/336 = 0.143 < 0.25); at fewer than 192 running samples the share exceeds 0.25 and they would engage. Fix wanted: replace the sentence with the latch/floor argument (the separate store is right, its justification is the latch bypass and the low percentile), and state the window-size condition.

5. **harness — the null control does not move.** `probe_duty.py:6` gives the plan `power_schedule=[0.0]`, so its "null: plan action 5.0 kW" arm prints duty `'idle'` — the same outcome class as the positive arm, so it cannot separate the arbiter's sysid path from a plan step. A control that moves: the same harness with `power_schedule=[5.0]` and a configured band prints duty `'space'`, and `flow_setpoint_for_level` maps 5.0 kW to the ceiling (55.0 °C) against 37.5 °C at 1.5 kW of 5.0. Fix wanted: run the control at a nonzero plan level with a band and keep it beside the sysid arm in the fixer's test.
