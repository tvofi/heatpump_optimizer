R1 (architecture-unsound, harness) design.md §1 "Shared helpers" and Notes item 1. The note partitions sections without the repository's own measured dependency graph between sections. `tests/case_select.py` (merged in #3896) records, in `tests/case_closures.json` scripts["tests/features.py"].spans[*].needs, every earlier span that a span depends on: a name it binds, a module attribute it patched, or process state it moved (RNG, env, sys.path). That is 10241 edges over 248 spans. Measured against the note's partition, 8206 of those edges cross a seam, and 245 of 248 spans have at least one need in another part. The note's join rule covers def/class closures only ("108 def/class names"). It leaves executed top-level bindings undecided. Examples: `_PA_IDS = itertools.count()` (actuation section at :65312) is read by :89128, which also reads surface's `_none = _SavLedger()`. :70779 reads model's `_opt234`/`_h234` (:12931) and actuation's `_go = _ghc_coord()` (:44107). :93491 reads plan's `_BO_TZ` and model's `_br_params`. My directional simulation of the join does not reach a fixpoint after 49 passes (5 sections oscillate). The patches the note counts as "53 or 56, unreconciled" are this same class. Fix wanted: build the partition on case_select's `needs`, separating name-only bindings (move to shared or duplicate) from state-moving edges (same part, or rebuilt per part). Make the rule total, with a tie-break for multi-seam needs. Re-measure the table after joins, and state the join and move counts. Carry: dismissed: revise point, owned by the designer's next note

R3 (architecture-unsound) design.md §4 items 2-3. The port is not local. The rule is global: module-majority with seam-order ties, cross-seam helper moves and joins. A branch hunk that adds one module reference or one cross-seam use can therefore reassign sections or helpers the branch did not write. That contradicts "the branch's parts differ from main's only by its own hunks". The killed_by values of main's rows for moved sections then go stale, and no drive runs. Fix wanted: the splitter commits a manifest (section title or hash -> part; helper -> shared) at the split. Port and classify modes keep every existing assignment from the manifest and classify only new sections. The port then re-drives the ledger rows of any section whose part changed.
