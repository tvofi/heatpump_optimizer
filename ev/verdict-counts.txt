38:## P186: `merge_pr.sh` has no carry mode for a verdicted head moved by automatic merges (S6-V106)
39:Verdict: REJECT. The binding cost is a second carry path. `merge_train.py` already lands exactly this case, and the train was the route the instance should have taken. A one-entry queue (`merge_train.py run`) or `merge_train.py add <pr> <verdict>` onto the live train both work. Step 3 runs `app_approve.sh --carry <verdict> <head>` and requires `CARRY: yes` (`merge_train.py:54`, `:1698`). A `--carry-from` mode would copy `app_approve --carry`, the verdict-comment reuse and the raise-hold judgement into a second script. That is two implementations of one gate to keep in step, for one instance.
