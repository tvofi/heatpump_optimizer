## Revise point
1. Shape and evidence plan: the two sides of the energy figure are not computed over the same window, and the branch is chosen by exact float equality across them (design.md section 2, "Read off the measured pair `live` and `sim`", "difference exactly 0", "the live side of a what-if is the scheduled plan's result"; coordinator.py:11172, :11180, :11190, :11223, :6318-6320, :6396, :3483-3485; const.py:1588; optimizer.py:5665).
   - `live` at coordinator.py:11223 is `self._optimization_result` (:11172), solved at its own `plan_step_start(now)` on its own `_forecast_arrays(now)` (:6318-6320) and replaced only by a successful solve (:6396). `sim` is solved at the what-if's instant on arrays anchored at the what-if's quarter (:11180, :11190; the grid is 15 min, :7404-7413). The plan is re-made every `optimization_interval`, 30 min by default (:3483-3485, const.py:1588), and not at all in comfort, boost or off mode (:6020-6022). So on the panel's path the plan is one or more steps older than the what-if for about half of all asks at the default interval, and by any number of steps after a failed solve or in a fixed mode.
   - Then the two "first 24 h" windows are offset, and the energy difference carries the weather of the steps that left the window against those that entered it. That term is not small against the signal: the note's day-window ask is 0.099 kWh a day, and one quarter-hour step at a few kelvin of day-to-day forecast difference is of that order. The sign of a day-window ask is then the forecast's, not the ask's.
   - The branch fails the same way and silently. With offset windows the energies of an ask that changes no schedule (the panel's hot-water, quiet-window or wood-slot change with comfort untouched; a zero ask) are unequal, so it takes the schedule branch: it is priced as the window term times the mean price and the pair is never solved. On the background path the windows agree only while the tile's or advisor's `dt_util.now()` falls in the solve's own quarter (the calls follow the solve in one task, :6417-6425, :8945-8947, :10673-10675; the comment at :11176-11179 names the straddle); when it does not, a cap figure reads the window term and is folded into the tile's 30-day mean. Even inside one quarter, on the user path the reference of each side starts from `_thermostat_held_state` over that side's measured state (optimizer.py:5665, wood side and buffer kept as measured, :576-620), and the two measured states are not one object: "exactly 0" then rests on a second pass erasing a float difference.
   - The evidence plan cannot see any of this. Every row of section 1, check (a), the probe and all eight mutants run `async_run_optimization` and `async_simulate` at one frozen instant, on an outdoor series that is flat at -5; "a zero ask reads +0.0 (12 of 12, both branches)" and "spread by start 0 by construction" hold at offset 0 only. The perturbation the finding names is the start; the plan's age is the start's sibling and has no row.
   Fix wanted: (i) both energies and the mean price computed at the what-if's instant over the what-if's arrays, the live configuration's by the same two thermostat passes on a scratch optimizer, no solve added and nothing read off `_optimization_result` for this key; with that the two result fields need not be written on every scheduled solve, and the passes leave the scheduled solve's wall. (ii) the branch chosen by what the ask changes (the two solves' comfort targets compared, or the two energies once (i) makes them the same computation over the same inputs), stated in the note. (iii) in check (a) and the carrier harness: the what-if asked one step and two steps after the plan was solved, on an outdoor series that differs between the horizon's two days; the zero ask reads 0.0 there, a hot-water-only ask and the cap ask reach the pair branch (solve count 3 then 2), and each panel ask stays on its side of 0. (iv) a mutant that reads the live energy off the scheduled result, red on (iii).
