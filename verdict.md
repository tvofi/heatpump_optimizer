## Revise points
1. design.md §1 (steady_start), architecture-unsound: the step-0 target is re-derived as `config.get_comfort_temp(start)`, a second derivation of a quantity optimizer.py already owns. The plan's comfort target comes from `_build_comfort_bounds` over `utc_step_starts` hours with `when=step_datetimes[i]` (optimizer.py:3415-3433, 3756-3763). The start settlement's held state reads `_baseline_target(comfort_targets, 0, target_temp)` (optimizer.py:5406-5409). The note's whole "settled" argument rests on "from held starts the start side of the settlement is zero", and that holds only while the two targets are equal by construction. A copy can drift on the hour basis, on `when`, or on weekend and holiday overrides. Fix wanted: steady_start takes its target from the same builder the solve uses (one shared step-0 accessor over `_build_comfort_bounds`'s inputs, or a held-start flag on SolveInputs that optimize resolves itself), never from a re-call of get_comfort_temp. Add a mutant to §5 that offsets the steady_start target and must turn the start-settlement-zero check red.
2. design.md §2 and §5 (no feedback into the plan), architecture-unsound / harness: the note never names the optimizer instance or the state the live-side steady solve uses. The live plan solves on `_warm_seeded(self, record.optimizer())` (coordinator.py:6169-6172). The in-process fallback of `_await_optimize` mutates the instance it is given (coordinator.py:2132-2134; optimize writes `_dhw_requirement`, `_price_known`, `_pv_surplus` and `_initial_buffer_temp` on self). The worker-fallback streak is keyed per entry and shared with the live solve (coordinator.py:2121-2127). Because the steady path triples what-if solves, a failing what-if now spends the live plan's fallback cap three times as fast. Fix wanted: state that the live steady solve runs on a fresh scratch HeatPumpOptimizer built from deep copies of config and params (or `base.config` and `base.params`), never on the record's or a warm-seeded instance. State that its result and the steady cache never reach `_optimization_result`, the warm seed or any SolveRecord, and decide whether what-if solves bump the per-entry fallback streak (`decision: orchestrator` if that changes #783's semantics). Put a no-feedback check in §5: the live record, `_optimization_result` identity and warm seed are unchanged after a `steady=True` what-if, and a mutant that solves the live side on the record's optimizer turns it red.
