## Revise points (each a defect in the design itself)

1. architecture-unsound. Note B "Guards", sysid.py:290 `_predict_step_excursion_plant`: the pre-check predicts one constant level, the schedule's mean, for 2.5 h with no relax phase, at commanded kW. That is not conservative in three ways. (a) The schedule 0.5, 1.0, 0.25, 0.75, 0.0 runs above its mean for the first hour, so the cumulative heat peaks above what the mean predicts. (b) The probe's own sizing counts the relax overshoot (sysid.py:1340-1366); this pre-check drops it. (c) The heat is computed from the commanded level, and the commanded level is the map under test: RESULT section 2 measured +1.7 to +2.3 kW over the command at the floor end. Fix: predict along the piecewise schedule plus `relax_hours`. The function already loops over (q, hours) pairs. Bound each level's heat by the draw the install can deliver there (the band top, or the window's observed draw), not by the command, and check both signs. Re-run the measure-first preset table under that predictor. The live `_over_excursion` abort stays the backstop.
2. architecture-unsound. Note B "Hot water keeps priority ... the arbiter serves a both step by its split" contradicts the tree. pump_arbiter.py:1351-1371 `_planned_duty` (the experiment branch at :1366-1371) gives any experiment the duty "space" or "idle" ("Space only -- a hot-water charge would share the meter"), so hot water is suspended for the whole step phase, 2.5 h. A held disinfection is the one exception (`_disinfecting`, :1315). Fix: state the suspension and bound it. Either the start defers when the plan asks for hot water inside the run's span, or a tank-floor stand-down applies. Delete the "fewer space-only minutes, no retry" reasoning: every sweep pass is space duty.
3. class-open. Learner contamination is half-designed. The routing invariant protects only `DrawRange`. coordinator.py:5314 `_learn_measured_cop` → `_fold_measured_cop` has no experiment gate, and neither has `_cop_fold_blocked` (:897-922). The capacity envelope hangs off that tail (the `_cop_fold_blocked` docstring says so). On P1's install a floor-level sweep interval would fold drawn/asked ≈ 5 as efficiency evidence wherever the judgement reads "follows", and the note keeps the window, so the judgement sees what it sees today. Leaving this as fixer note n1 leaves it open. Fix: design the exclusion in one place on the predecessor's seam. `MeasuredCop`'s judgement reads the interval's captured kind, which the pending prediction already captures beside `mode` (coordinator.py:10126), the way `draw_range.fold` takes `experiment_s` (:10033). Choose its refusal code against the predecessor's no-new-literal rule. Null control: a sweep run leaves `_cop_samples`, the COP scale and the envelope byte-identical to the same run without the sweep. Re-count the coordinator ratchet with this in it.
4. harness (size figure wrong). The note claims in F.1 that "the replay window does not shrink on P1's install". It leaves out two things. (a) A's 1008 rows at the download's indent 2 (`download_bytes`, debugger.py:363): measured at 280-385 KB depending on how origin and duty are coded, not the 84 KB compact. (b) A full 168 h collection: P1's bundle spans 141.5 h, with 6 snapshots of about 234 KB and 561 rows. The non-replay budget is 8,388,608 − 262,144 − 3,375,297 = 4,751,167 bytes. The need is about 3,663,331 + 660,000 + ~340,000 + ~300,000 ≈ 4.96 MB, so the replay falls to the next `REPLAY_SPANS` entry, which is 6 h, not 24 h (debugger.py:85-86). The trace's open day "as float arrays" is also unpriced at indent 2 (1440 × ~40 cells). Fix: encode the map rows like the trace (columnar strings), price every addition at indent 2 and at 168 h, and state the drop order when over the cap (trace, snapshots or replay first), with the manifest line that says which dropped.
5. harness (cost and gate claims false). (a) "5.625 kWh, an upper bound on extra cost" is commanded energy. The note's own source measured drawn ≈ 1.80 × asked (median) and +2 kW at the floor end, so the true bound is the draw ceiling: 2.5 h × p_max = 10 kWh on P1's band. The "flat-price null: the cost figure is that product" is then a tautology, because it re-checks the arithmetic, not the bound. Fix: state the bound on the band top and publish the metered energy of the run in its result reason. The null is that the published figure equals the meter's integral over the run on a flat price. (b) `conditions_met` (sysid.py:1527-1561) gates price and hour only at the ARMED-to-SETTLING step. So "never runs into a peak" and "Runtime 3.5 h, inside the 23-05 window" are false for any start after 01:30: a 04:xx start runs to about 08:00. Fix: gate the start on `end_hour` minus the run, and on the prices across the run's span, or restate both claims as the fit's existing behaviour.
6. harness (the conclusiveness claim has no yield measurement). Under a level hold the written set-point tracks the inlet, because floor = inlet + lift (thermal_model.py:1762-1783). RESULT shows open-loop partial segments held 2-9 min. Section A's rule (close on any set-point change, drop the first 3 min, keep at least 2) therefore fragments every level below 1.0. At P1's 42 °C running inlet the whole band spans 3 K of set-point (floor 52, heat 55) on the entity's grid. "Ten open-loop steady points" is asserted, not shown. Fix: measure first, on a synthetic plant over P1's inlet range of 32-42 °C, the steady minutes and distinct written set-points per level. Then decide from the numbers whether a sweep's passes segment by level (carrying written min, mean and max) rather than by set-point. Have the result reason report the set-point span and the steady minutes, so a collapsed sweep is visible, not counted.
7. class-open. F says a D15 seat "could then replay the integration's inputs and writes for the whole week at minute resolution". No instrument in Part F consumes the trace: `tools/replay/debug_replay.py` (D15 step 5) reads the replay half only, and config-key columns need the entry's mapping to become state rows. Fix: add the trace as an input to debug_replay.py in Part F, with its own synthetic arm in dbg_bundle_gen.py, or withdraw the claim.
