## Decisions (decision: owner; recommendation, no revise point)
- Switch-on during the hold: keep "ON passes". When the switch already reads on, ON writes nothing, so it adds no churn. It matters only after a person or the device switched the unit off. The evidence shows the unit's own backup heater running through the latched fault, so a switch held off would remove the only heat. This is also today's behaviour after a manual off, and it matches frost's "on wins".
- 10 minutes as a constant: keep the constant. The longest self-clearing fault in the evidence lasts a few minutes, and the 20-40 s protection trips are far below the threshold. The wait costs at most about ten set-point writes and one mode write per episode, against hours of churn. A false hold on a longer self-clearing fault costs a short suspension and a repair that clears itself. An option would add config_flow.py lines at zero headroom. Revisit this if another install reports a longer self-clearing fault; the opt-out covers a misfit meanwhile.
- Order (decision: orchestrator): the note names FROST-HOLD as its only predecessor. FLOWCAP (which lands first by ruling) and STEP-KICK also edit pump_arbiter.py, and FLOWCAP edits config_flow.py and const.py too. Place this group after all three, and re-measure the ratchet at its merge base.
