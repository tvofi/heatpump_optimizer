## Decisions routed

- `decision: owner`: with both sources configured, the note lets a reading sensor win over the manual option even where the sensor reads higher. That follows the ruling literally ("if there is no such sensor"). The alternative is the lower of the sensor and the option whenever both read, which is the only reading under which "never write above the limit the user typed" holds. My recommendation is the lower of the two, because it is cheaper to undo and safer for the device. The data_description text must follow whichever is ruled.
