Consequences:
- (a) In Home Assistant the restore fails. The fixture's stub service accepts 18, but `inputs.write_setpoint` calls with `blocking=True` and does no clamp (inputs.py:597-616), and HA's `number.set_value` and `input_number.set_value` refuse a value outside min/max. That refusal is from reading HA, not measured here. The restore is then latched and the key stays armed for good: every later off retries, and `async_forget` tells the user to "set it to 18 by hand", a value the entity cannot hold.
