Design review note — concept 4

Mechanism
The box outline is the type system: 2-unit accent stroke and a 3-unit accent tab for the heat pump and the valve, 1.5-unit ink stroke and an ink tab for stores, 1.5-unit dashed for emitters, 1.5-unit dotted accent for outside air. The wood coil is drawn on the box itself as a spring on the wood tank's right border, and its pipe is the one dashed accent pipe. A key strip below the drawing decodes the four treatments.
Cost
No height at all; one key strip (about 26 px at 1280, two rows at 375). Every box keeps its exact current title and row geometry, so the change is one class per box plus two decorative paths.
Risk
Stroke weight in this card already means something else: pipes are 1.5 and the editing surface widens pipes to 3.5 to say "clickable". A 2-unit box outline can read as focus or edit state. Dashed and dotted outlines at phone scale shimmer, and the four treatments are four things to learn — the same vocabulary cost as concept 1 with less literal recognition.
At 375 px
Patterns survive scaling better than glyphs; the 2-unit stroke stays visible at the 560 px floor. The spring on the border is 24 units tall, about 19 px, and is the smallest coil mark of the five.
Wood-coil answer
The coil becomes part of the box's own outline — the one concept where the tank is literally drawn as a tank with a coil — and the coil's pipe is distinguishable from every heating pipe. That is the cleanest answer to "the coil pipe looks like a heating pipe".
