Harmony 1 · The dialog type floor

The enlarged dialog sets its own base font from its width, between 12 and 21 px. At every common width that is 12–12.9 px — smaller than the card's own 14 px base — so the same stats, legend and captions render smaller in the enlarged view than on the tile, and the smallest roles fall under the card's own smallest type step, the 12 px --hpo-text-xs. The proposal floors the dialog base at the card's base and floors every caption role at 12 px.

The Plan page, wholethe base and the legend note

Before: dialog base 12.0 px at 375/768 and 12.9 px at 1280 (measured in the browser); the legend note is 0.76 em of that — 9.1–9.8 px above 600 px wide. At 375 px the card's phone rule already raises the legend to 1rem (16 px, note 12.16 px), so on a phone it is the dialog chrome and the page captions that carry the 12 px base. After: base 14 px at every width up to the 21 px cap; caption roles are max(12 px, 0.85 em).

The what-if panel, alonethe page's smallest type lives here

At 375 the Plan page has 21 CSS text nodes below 12 px and 17 of them are in this panel; above 600 px the count is 22 (the legend note joins at 9.1–9.8 px) and 17 are still here. Before: the group titles are 0.9 em of the base and the hints 0.85 em. After: the hint role is max(12 px, 0.85 em); the titles clear 12 px once the base is 14.

What this does not touch

  • The chart's axis labels are not in this ramp: they are drawn in viewBox units and floored separately at MIN_AXIS_FONT_PX = 8 (measured 8.0 px at a 375 px dialog). That floor is deliberate and stays.
  • On the tile the same caption roles measure 10.6 px (legend note), 11.5 px (caveat) and 11.9 px (zoom buttons) on the 14 px base. The same max(12px, …) rule moves them by 0.1–1.4 px; it is the same finding, one surface over.
  • The 21 px cap and the 0.0105 width ratio are unchanged; at a 1700 px dialog the base is 17.85 px.
