Harmony 3 · Sibling surfaces and states

Three places where a page disagrees with its sibling for no design reason: the Health and Advisor rows take their surface from a Home Assistant theme variable while their text comes from the card's own literal (measured 1.2:1 in dark when that variable is absent); the Health section headings are the card's only 700 weight where its other headings are 600; and the Savings empty state is a left-aligned primary-ink paragraph where the other empty states are a centred muted panel.
