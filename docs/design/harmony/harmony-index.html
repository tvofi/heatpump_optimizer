Harmony — the card's design voice, page by page

The card declares its own design system in two token tables and writes every rule against it. This study states that voice as a checkable list, enumerates every page and view from the card's own render paths, measures each page against the voice in a real browser at 375/768/1280 in light and dark, and prototypes four changes. The full argument, evidence and file:line anchors are in HARMONY.md; this page is the index.

The voice in twelve lines

V1 · One base, 14 px

The card inherits the host's 14 px. The enlarged dialog must not render a role smaller than the tile. Its base is 12–12.9 px until the dialog itself is 1333 px wide.

V2 · Ramp 12/13/15/18

--hpo-text-xs…lg. Headings 600, dialog chrome 500, never 700. Measured on the face: labels 12, chips 13, headline 15, number/title 18.

V3 · Captions 0.76–0.85 em

Footnotes, not a second ramp — and never below 12 px on an HTML surface, the ramp's smallest step.

V4 · A drawing is not text

Chart and setup map draw in viewBox units. The chart floors rendered axis text at 8 px; the map floors by a 560 px phone min-width. The ramp does not apply inside a drawing.

V5 · One surface/ink pair, one switch

A theme variable may be the first choice only where the ink is the theme's too. Otherwise the card's literal pair wins.

V6 · Status is a dot and a word

ok/warn/crit each carry a text and a background token; never colour alone.

V7 · Space 4/8/12/16/24

Card padding 12; dialog 16 (12 at ≤600 px); page 0.5 em × 0.25 em.

V8 · Radii 6/12/pill

Plus the dialog chip's 1.3 em and the map's 10-unit box radius. The drawn Configure control claims the legend chip's label size; it does not hold (measured).

V9 · Strokes 1/1.5/2/3.5

Outlines and dividers; icons and pipes; focus ring; editing pipes. Colour by role, never weight.

V10 · Targets 24 / 44 px

Every pointer / coarse, with SVG targets converted through unitsPerPx.

V11 · One decorative motion

The chart view-controls' 120 ms fade, off under prefers-reduced-motion.

V12 · One empty-state shape

.empty — muted, centred, 28 px 12 px. Three pages use it; Savings is the exception.

No change needed

  • The chart's 8 px axis floor and its viewBox units; the setup map's scaled type and its 560 px phone min-width.
  • The phone legend's 1rem (16 px) and the tabs' 1.19 em — both are chrome sized for touch, not body text.
  • The 0.0105 width ratio, the tile's 13 px chip and 18 px title, the 6 px chip gap, the 14 px shared radius, the pill.
  • The map's 10-unit box radius and 1/1.5/3.5-unit strokes; the status pairs; the single 120 ms fade.
  • The Health page's absence of an empty state: its checklist is how the page says what is missing.
  • The em-based dialog chrome itself — the mechanism is right; it needs the base floored, not replaced.

Small corrections (one line each, no prototype)

  • C1 — var(--hpo-text-sm, 14px) ×3 in RECEIPT_CSS; the token is 13 px. Latent, not visual.
  • C2 — the setup chip comment's claim of "the same label size a legend chip wears" is false by width (8.6–18.2 vs 12–12.9).
  • C3 — DIALOG_FONT_PX_MAX = 21 is unreachable: the dialog width caps at 1700 → 17.85 px.
