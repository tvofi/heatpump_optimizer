R10-SAVINGS-NEGATIVE. A production regression in v7.0.1. The owner reported that the monthly savings entity dropped to extreme negative values after the upgrade from v7.0.0. Leaves #201 open.

**Root cause.** #2411 (commit ee38beda2, `MonthlyLedger._coverage_factor` in `ledger.py`) scales the open month by the month's hours over the hours its `settled_hours` meta settled. It took that meta whenever its sum was positive. v7.0.0 never wrote the meta. A store upgraded mid-month gains it at the first v7.0.1 settlement, so the month has weeks of lines and only minutes of coverage. Every energy-proportional figure in the open row (`savings_months`, which the monthly savings sensor publishes) was then multiplied by `month_hours / settled`, which is thousands where the calendar-day ratio was below 2. The sign comes from the saving booked under v7.0.0 for the month so far. That saving was negative in the owner's month. It is the October negative month that #2411's item 1 addresses for new bookings, and the multiplier blew it up. The docstring already said such a month falls back to the calendar-day ratio. The predicate tested that the meta existed, not that it began with the month.

The owner's diagnostics export was read locally only. The store's open-month coverage meta holds a single fold, against the spot samples of the whole month so far. Through the production ledger, the export's open row reads a factor in the thousands at origin/main and the calendar-day ratio at this head. No value from the export is in this tree or this body. The fixture and the harness are synthetic.

**Fix.** The ledger owns the rule, in `_coverage_factor`. The coverage is the month's own only if it began with the month's first settlement. `observe_spot_and_settle_savings` folds one coverage sample (every interval with a positive length, which the coordinator's settlement guarantees: 0.05 to 2 h) and at most one spot sample (priced intervals only) in the same call. So complete coverage has at least as many folds as the month has spot samples. A month booked before the meta existed has fewer, and it falls back to `pro_rata_factor`, exactly like a month with no meta. `SPOT_PRICE_META` names the key that the call and the report already shared as a literal.

Alternatives considered:
- Seeding the meta with the elapsed hours at its first fold. This writes a guess into the store, and it cannot repair stores v7.0.1 has already written, because those already hold the meta.
- A versioned store migration that drops the meta. The next fold re-creates it mid-month, so the defect comes back. A migration still needs this reader rule.
- Capping the factor. This hides the inconsistency and would also cap a genuine fresh install.

The reader rule handles every case, including stores v7.0.1 has already written, and needs no migration. The upgraded month reads the calendar-day ratio until it closes. The next month is measured from its first settlement.
