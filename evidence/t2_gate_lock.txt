44	That single lease serialises the commands that must run ALONE -- those that
45	measure the machine, so their numbers are comparable only with nothing else
46	running: ``stress.py`` and ``harness_headers.py``. Commands that are
47	multi-minute CPU-bound but do not measure take instead from the heavy-slot
48	POOL (``take_slot``/``release_slot``, ``heavy_slots()`` slots under the `slots`
49	subdirectory; #2267), which is what makes nudge.md's four-prepr cap mechanical
50	instead of advisory. ``needs_lease`` classifies a scope into the three answers
51	``run.sh`` dispatches on: ``lease``, ``slot`` or ``none``.
52	
53	A pool slot is held for the LIFE of its run, not for its lease: the taker's pid
54	is recorded and an expired slot is freed only when that pid is gone, because
55	under load a prepr routinely runs past ``LEASE_SECONDS`` and expiry-stealing
56	admitted a fifth while the first still ran. The exclusive lease keeps its
57	expiry rule, since a stress run measures the machine and must not be extended
58	past a waiter. ``slot-status`` counts both witnesses of an over-subscribed
59	pool: holders alive past their lease, and runs that could not take a slot at
60	all (``slot-unleased``).
