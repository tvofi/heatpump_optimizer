=== R10-CEILING-PROBE ===
Ordered by tvofi 2026-10-19: fold a deliberate ceiling-set-point probe into the cheap-night system-identification experiment (sysid.py, gated on mild outdoor temperature, cheap price, night window). Purpose: set-point-driven installs never command the ceiling, so the metered draw window is censored at the top and the plugin cannot learn the pump's real maximum (owner: configured 5 kW, a Rotenso Windmi 14 kW whose real electrical maximum is believed near 3 kW). The probe writes the ceiling flow set-point for a bounded step inside the experiment window, records drawn power against the written set-point into draw_range's ceiling-commanded evidence (R10-SETPOINT-BOOST fix B), and lets the band top be learned from uncensored samples. Controls: the experiment's existing comfort and price gates hold; a probe never runs outside them; a synthetic pump with a known maximum is recovered within tolerance; duty-cycling installs unchanged; flat-price null.

=== R10-DEMAND-OVERBOOK ===
Found by R10-SETPOINT-BOOST's demand analysis (tvofi 2026-10-19: the planner fills the whole day with heating; fixes must work on every install, without a heat meter). The plan books about 38 kWh/day electric for space while the metered October average is about 8.6 kWh/day for space plus hot water, roughly 8x over-booked. The learned UA implies far more loss than metered delivery supports; idle phantom draw explains only about 5%. Root-cause by replay on synthetic installs (never the owner's private export): UA learner bias, booked-vs-delivered heat, comfort weights. Then fix so planned demand matches delivered heat with no heat sensor. Controls: flat-price null, a synthetic install spread, mutation, and a DP reference for solver optimality (not run in SETPOINT-BOOST).

=== R10-BUFFER-STORE ===
Found by R10-SETPOINT-BOOST's census: with a 750 L manual-valve buffer configured, the planner publishes the same plans as with no buffer, and the tank stays at its start temperature, so the buffer is never used as a heat store for cheap windows. This affects the owner's install type. Root-cause on synthetic installs, then make the buffer's storage credit drive pre-heating in cheap windows within the comfort band. Controls: flat-price null, buffer and no-buffer arms, mutation.

=== R10-COP-PARTLOAD ===
Found by R10-SETPOINT-BOOST's demand analysis. The COP model keys only on outdoor temperature and the tank/weather-curve flow; it has no power (part-load) dependence and no burst term. So the planner cannot weigh tvofi's hypotheses (2026-10-19): power near the floor is suboptimal under price swings, and a short high-power burst does not raise the flow temperature enough to hurt COP much. Measure first on synthetic installs: COP vs compressor power at a fixed lift, and the flow-temperature rise during a short burst. Add a model term only if the measured effect is material, with controls: a flat-price null, a no-term null that keeps plans byte-identical, and mutation. Must work without a heat meter.
