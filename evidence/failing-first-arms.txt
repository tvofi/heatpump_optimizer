  FAIL a policy head whose owner approval at this exact head cites a mandate in force lands
  FAIL a policy head with NO mandate is refused as before -- the approval is at the head, so the refusal names the mandate, not the review (the fallback is the point)
  FAIL a policy head with a mandate but no owner approval at its head is refused, named as the review
  FAIL a mandate whose window has passed behaves as no mandate (expiry arm)
  FAIL a revoked mandate refuses the policy head, named as revoked
  FAIL a mandate scoped budget-raise does not cover policy merging
  FAIL policy content that moved after the owner's review is refused and named as that change
  FAIL an approval not at this exact head is refused as that, when the policy blobs did not move
  FAIL batch fallback: a policy head with no owner approval at its head stops admission before any proof is spent (the no-mandate fallback, on the batch side)
  FAIL batch mandate: the same head is admitted and merges, its approval and mandate RE-READ at the merge itself (admission reads, land reads again)
