What changed. With the Tibber source (any `price_source` other than `entity`, which is how `coordinator._entity_price_source` decides it) and no `--price-entity`, `build_export` takes the price sensor from `<config>/.storage/core.entity_registry` (`tibber_price_candidates`): an enabled `sensor.*` entry of platform `tibber` whose unique id has no underscore and whose `translation_key` is `electricity_price`, or absent (an older registry). It keeps only candidates with a numeric state in force during the window (read through the existing `read_states`). The entry's own `price_entity` breaks a tie between homes when it names one of the candidates. Otherwise exactly one candidate must remain, or the export refuses with a message that lists every candidate and says to pass `--price-entity`. An explicit `--price-entity` always wins, and the `entity` source is unchanged. The chosen sensor is printed on stderr. The code is stdlib only and opens no socket.
