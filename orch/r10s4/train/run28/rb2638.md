## Change

- `definesJob(text, id)`:
  - The job `id` is defined when `  <id>:` opens it.
  - It is only called, not defined, when its own body carries a job-level `    uses:`. The body is the lines indented under the job, up to the next two-space key.
  - A step's `- uses:` sits deeper, so it is not a call.
- `contractWorkflowFiles(root)` lists the definers, sorted.
- `assertAcceptance` uses `contractWorkflowFiles()` in place of the inline text match. "Exactly one definer" stays the rule: none, or two, still refuses.
- Both functions are exported, and `tests/entities.py` pins them.
