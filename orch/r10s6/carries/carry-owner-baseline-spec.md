Owner specification for the savings baseline (tvofi, 2026-10-24, binding acceptance criterion). Actual consumption is calculated from the heat pump's power sensor. The baseline must use power levels similar to the ones recorded, simulated as a plain thermostat over the exact same price profile, for both space heating and DHW. The DHW baseline assumes the DHW thermostat is always on: no DHW windows. Savings are the cost of that baseline minus the metered actual cost, priced over the same profile. Under one flat price, the volume difference may remain, but timing savings must be 0. The card's Savings tab equals ledger.savings_months cell for cell.
