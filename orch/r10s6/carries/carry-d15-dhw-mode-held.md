D15 production triage (2026-10-24, owner bundle, 7.0.4). Control defect: on a plan-idle step the pump arbiter writes no mode (`desired`, pump_arbiter.py:700-707), so a DHW mode the integration set for a planned DHW slot stays on through the idle steps that follow. The owner expects the mode to return to space-only or off. Evidence: in the 57-step pump-duty ledger, 7 of 8 plan-idle steps within 3 steps after a DHW slot ran; null control: 4 of 41 idle steps with no DHW slot in the previous 3 steps ran. The pump also charges the tank past the one booked 15-minute step, so the plan books less DHW energy than the pump draws, which feeds the savings volume gap (see R10-SAV-COMFORT carry d15-sav-volume-2). Fix: on the step after a DHW slot ends, the arbiter writes the planned non-DHW mode, or the plan books the real tank-charge duration. Pin both with a fixture replaying a DHW slot followed by idle steps, and a mutation that drops the post-slot mode write.
