After R10-LIVESTATE, rebinding the coordinator's _ctx is how live state is published (CoordinatorContext is immutable; the coordinator publishes by rebinding through one context method). This group's "make the context write-once" item therefore means the CoordinatorContext instance, not the _ctx binding; a write-once binding would refuse every live-state publish. _update_current_state's mixing_valve_target and ambient_humidity writes stay for this group as the parameter owner.
