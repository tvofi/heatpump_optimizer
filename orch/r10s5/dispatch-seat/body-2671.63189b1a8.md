An obligation was a level read out of append-only history. The relay appended OWED rows to `cloud-relay.log`, and the tick replayed them, keyed on a PR number, so a closer could match only a PR's `POSTED`. This PR adds the obligations ledger from the approved design (`handoff/orch-machinery` `09174d94e`, `dev/audit/design/orch-machinery/DESIGN.md` §6.3), revised against current main as the brief asks, plus the orphaned-handoff RCA's extension (`dev/audit/rca/N-orphaned-handoff.md` §5).

- **`tools/audit/seat/ledger.py`** is the one module. It holds one table of kinds and two stores beside the relay's log, in the directory `<log>.level` already uses. `dispositions.tsv` (`<ref> <sha> <utc> <how>`) is written only by `dispose`. `events.tsv` (`open`/`close`/`attempt`) is append-only. There is one fold.
  - A **derived** kind is recomputed every pass and never stored. The relay rewrites `handoff-no-pr`, `handoff-update`, `probe-ref`, `handoff-hold-malformed`, and #2664's `probe-refused` and `confirm-owed` into `<log>.level`; the tick computes `design-build` and `review-owed`.
  - An **event** kind is keyed on kind, subject and sha: `reviewer-fire`, `verdict-unposted`, `approve-held-runs`, `dispatch`, `train-stop`, and `postmerge-owed` (K17 (2)). An `open` at a new sha supersedes the subject's entry: a moved subject closes the old entry and opens one. A `close` at the entry's sha is the producer's disposal.
  - The PR-keyed events also close, inside the fold, on what the tick reads: the PR's head moved off the sha, the PR is no longer open (`subject-absent`), or a **read-back**. A read-back is an approver `Fix review:` comment whose first line is the relayed verdict (`verdict-unposted`, K17 (4b), `comment-readback.md`), or one that names the sha (the other PR-keyed kinds). The relay's `POSTED` is a relayed claim and closes nothing.
  - Refusals: a ref of no arm, a kind of no table, and a derived kind opened as an event. A missing store is a first run. An unreadable store, or a row of the wrong shape or of no kind, is UNREADABLE and never a clean board.
  - The import runs once: `cloud-disposed` with the four prefixes the relay's `dispose` takes (probe, handoff, handoff-body, and review since #2664; review/ is the `confirm-owed` arm), taking the last disposition per ref, and the log's still-open OWED lines under b38c6431a's rule. A reader such as `board` or the tick folds the import in memory and writes nothing.
  - Exit codes for `board`, `level` and `disposed`: 0 nothing owed, 1 owed (with `board`'s summary line), 2 refused or unreadable. A finding is never an instrument failure (RCA §5.6).
- **`level`** is the handoff level. A `handoff/` or `handoff-body/` ref is owed unless one of these holds: it is a state ref by name (`audit-r<N>-plan`, `audit-r<N>-roster`); its topic has an open PR; it is disposed at its sha; or main already carries the change. "Carries" means one of two things. Either the code tip is inside main or a live `fix/*` head (**ancestry**). Or every line its uncovered commits add is on main's copy of that file, and every line they remove is gone from it (**content**: the `ecaf178c` case, a hunk another PR re-applied into a file main had since changed). Each owed line carries `ct=<epoch>` and the last opener attempt at that tip.
- **Aging** (`escalate`) turns stranded handoffs into a queue. DISPATCH lines come oldest first, one per topic, naming the roster group (`roster_lib.group_for_branch`) and the repair (`opener`, or the missing ref). An OWNER FLAG is added past the second threshold. The switch is `HPO_LEDGER_AGING=<dispatch_h>,<owner_h>` or `off`. The default is the owner's ruling of 2026-10-21 in carry 13: 4 h and 12 h (R14). See `## Forward-carry` for what that default prints today.
- **`cloud_relay.sh`** writes through the ledger. A push-verdict that passed opens `verdict-unposted <pr> <head> <first line>`. `reviewer-fire`, `approve-held-runs` and `dispatch` open events: approve and dispatch are **their own kinds**, which answers the round-3 review's N11-R (the failure file has no reason field to carry them). `handoff-update` is a level line from `cloud-handoff-failed`. `handoff-no-pr` is `ledger.py level`'s output. `dispose` and `disposed` call the ledger. The log keeps only the `POSTED` journal: where #2664's relay appended `OWED <utc> <pr> <review-sha|-> <why>` after a failed or impossible confirm, this one prints `cloud_relay: UNPOSTED #<pr>: <why>` (same `<why>`, confirm's last output line) and the verdict stays owed in the ledger. A failed listing or an unreadable ledger leaves the level as it was.
- **`orchestrator_tick.py`**: `relay_owed` prints the level (a line of no ledger kind is REFUSED), the escalations, and the events folded against this pass's open-PR heads and read-back verdicts. `verdict_levels` collects the read-back and prints `REVIEW OWED: #N <head12>` (fold s4) for each open non-draft head that no approver verdict names and no `bus.sh` dispatch record carries. PLAN prints `DESIGN-BUILD:` (RCA §5.5) for an approved `design/<g>` (its `design-verdict/<g>` reads `Design review: approve <design tip>`) whose roster group is not dispatched.
- **`open_pr.sh`** records each attempt on its handoff ref: `started`, then `opened,PR=<n>` or `unopened` on every exit, a TERM's included. The 11:50Z `Terminated: 15` openers would show on their obligation. Bash's `$?` in an EXIT trap after a TERM is the last command's status, so the record names no signal; that is measured, in `## Figures`.
- **Wiring** lands with the instrument: `ledger.py --self-test` and `r10_ledger_mutants.py --self-test` are steps in `governance.yml`. The reason is that `pr-contract` makes the body answer a red on that wiring check (the round-3 review's N12-R correction: `instrument-self-tests` is not a required check). The two other round-3 figure corrections are honoured here: the approve and dispatch writers are counted as code paths (N13-R; at this head they are three `ledger_open` lines, see the companion harness), and nothing below restates the design's section-11 counts (N14-R).

Design choices a later seat could read as bugs:
- Event entries are keyed on (kind, subject), with the sha as state. Two legacy verdict OWEDs for one PR are therefore one obligation, the latest. The old tick printed both; its self-test arm is re-pinned to say so.
- The relay's `POSTED` closes nothing, not even a migrated row's `verdict-unposted`, apart from the import itself applying b38c6431a's rule to history. This departs from carry 35's literal "cleared by its POSTED" because K17 (4b), the later owner-approved amendment, rules that a relayed claim is not a close. Carry 35's other half, events keyed on their PR, holds.
- A refused event is printed and not stored, and the relay's pass goes on. The census that stops a new shape is the ledger self-test's check that every `level_line`/`ledger_open` kind in `cloud_relay.sh` (`relay_writers`, skipping comment lines and the helpers' own definitions) is a ledger kind, which turns CI red on the pull request that adds it. The relay does not stop at run time.
- A body ref follows its code ref for the ancestry and content closers. A body-only ref (no code ref) is owed until disposed.
- Alternatives that lost (fixer.md step 17): keeping the log as the event store kept the harness grammar, but it cannot hold (4b) (a `POSTED` would close `verdict-unposted`) and it keys on the PR alone. Deriving `verdict-unposted` from the comments alone loses the relayed first line the read-back must match. Making approve and dispatch reasons on `handoff-update` needs a reason field written after a successful update, which then poisons the failed file's retry key.
