## row-semantics.txt -> R10-SCORES and R10-ADVISOR
From R10-SAV-BASELINE (#2255, PR #2411): in each `ledger.savings_months` row, `actual_sek` is the month's bill (`MonthlyLedger.month_cost`, the receipt total over every billed line). `savings_sek` is thermostat minus plan over the intervals a plan covered, both priced at spot plus the interval's grid fee. `baseline_sek` = `actual_sek + savings_sek`. The open row is scaled by the month's hours over its settled hours (the `settled_hours` meta), with capacity unscaled. The horizon figure is the plan's `predicted_savings`, and its `deferred_energy_cost` is the end gap minus the start gap. Read these figures; do not recompute either.
