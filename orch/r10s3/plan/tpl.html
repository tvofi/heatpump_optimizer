How the ETAs are computed

Every figure on this page is plan.json's, written by tools/plan/replan.py; this renderer draws it and computes no schedule of its own. A wave is the set of groups dispatched at one moment, and the next opens as soon as one of {{slots}} pull-request slots frees, so a wave never waits for the previous one to drain; the cap limits concurrency, not the wave boundary. Duration is dispatch to merge by roster size, from tools/plan/durations.json: {{dur_sizes}}, the median lead time of done round-10 groups of that size (S is above M because small groups waited in review; XL has no merged sample and is 1.5 × L). A group whose fixer is already working gets {{live_pct}}% of its size duration as the remaining estimate. Those are measured lead times, not effort: review rounds and queueing are inside them, so the slot schedule is an upper bound on closure. The batching window is 0 minutes — merge as soon as a batch is ready — because closure is the first objective; the measured trade is in durations.json.
