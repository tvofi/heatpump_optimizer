Round-9 fix F4.1 (lane F4: inputs, constants, sysid, flow lift). Part of #1644 (P2); Fixes #1684 (N-staleness); Fixes #1673 (N-clamp-range); Part of #201.

Before: `InputReader.read` delivered the DS18B20 fault codes -127 and 85 degC as measurements on every temperature input; `inputs.age_of` read `last_updated` and accepted a future stamp while `InputReader` preferred `last_reported` and refused one; a report-on-change thermometer silent for over an hour made Indoor Temperature unavailable; the flow-lift bias stopped 15 K over a model curve that asks 22-28 C, so a 40-50 C plant was priced up to 37 % too efficient; and the sysid step was sized to exactly the bound that aborts it, so sensor noise aborted a third of experiments.

After: every temperature key has a physical window and a reading outside it is refused as `implausible` with no value; one stamp owner (`inputs.state_stamp`, with the signed age `inputs.state_age`) serves the reader and `age_of` alike; the slow-store temperatures (indoor, lower floor, floor return, buffer, DHW) stay fresh for `QUIET_STORE_MAX_AGE_MINUTES` (360); the bias may rise to `flow_lift.FLOW_SUPPLY_MAX_C` over the sample's curve and a supply past that ceiling is refused as a sample; the step is sized `sysid.SIZING_NOISE_HEADROOM_C` under the abort bound, falling back to the full bound where no step fits under it.

How: `inputs.py` gains `state_stamp`, `state_age` and `plausible_c`, and `_age_minutes`, `_age_gate` and `age_of` read them; `const.py` gains `QUIET_STORE_MAX_AGE_MINUTES`, the range constants and `INPUT_PLAUSIBLE_RANGE_C`; `flow_lift.FlowCurveBias.observe` and `from_dict` bound the hot side by `FLOW_SUPPLY_MAX_C`; `SystemIdentification._size_step_power` searches the headroom bound first. Coordinator callers of `age_of` are unchanged (both already refuse `None`).

Decisions a reviewer may contest, each measured below:
- **Quiet-store horizon 360 min.** Clears the finding's field gap (4 h 15 min between reports of a healthy probe). Cost: a probe that dies silently feeds the learners up to 6 h of flatline before `_learning_frozen` freezes them, where 60 min allowed 1 h. The harness's 480 min cell stays unavailable by design. The alternative, a learner-only short horizon, needs `coordinator._learning_frozen` (lane F1).
- **Windows refuse fault codes, not plant states.** 85 degC is refused in a room, outdoors and in a floor circuit (`FLOOR_WATER_RANGE_C`, 60 C) and kept in a tank or water circuit, where it is a real temperature; the finder's harness therefore reads 2 of 12 at head, the same 2 its own `--range` perturbation leaves.
- **Sizer fallback.** Under the 0.15 K headroom, no step fits on the two-zone typical_slab and (at the sizer's 33-point grid) light_new houses in the `#1524` rig; without the fallback those experiments never run (6 existing `#1524` checks go red, mutant M6 below).
