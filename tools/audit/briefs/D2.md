# D2 — Mathematical and physical sanity

Owner's brief: *"Mathematical and physical sanity — the optimizer's objective,
the thermal model, COP and derates, tariff and price arithmetic."*

Read `COMMON.md` first.

## What you are hunting

Equations that are wrong, units that do not cancel, identities that do not
hold, and physical bounds that are violated — each shown by an executed
identity with a stated tolerance, not by reading the code.

## Method

1. **Thermal model.** Energy conservation per step (stores' enthalpy change
   equals delivered heat minus losses, to 1e-9 kWh); dt-invariance (four
   15-minute steps versus one hour, with the tolerance the integrator's
   order predicts); scalar `simulate_step` versus `simulate_trajectory_batch`
   bit parity on space-only, DHW, valve, wood, coil, external-heat and PV
   inputs; monotonicity (more power never cools a store); the buffer cap
   clamps a rate, not a state; no store crosses a physical bound in any golden
   scenario.
2. **COP and derates.** COP monotone in outdoor and flow temperature, Carnot
   fraction within the stated band, every derate in [0, 1], defrost derate
   bounded and continuous at the band edges, capacity envelope never below
   demand-at-derate without a reason code.
3. **Objective.** `predicted_cost == Σ price·power·dt` on every golden
   fixture; the savings identity; the terminal credit's sign and magnitude
   versus a re-simulated continuation; every penalty term's scaling with
   `price_weight`; the gradient used by the solver versus a central finite
   difference at random points (relative error and where it is worst).
4. **Tariff and prices.** Folding 15-minute and hourly prices into steps,
   month boundaries, 23- and 25-hour DST days, the capacity tariff charging
   the monthly peak exactly once, grid-fee day/night and weekday windows,
   currency conversion and the PV export price applied piecewise.
5. **Estimators.** `sysid.identify` bias under white, quantised (0.1 °C) and
   drifting noise; the confidence gate versus the bias it admits; the COP and
   heat-loss learners' clamps.

## Traps

Five fixtures are non-reproducible across BLAS builds (the `may-drift` set);
do not report last-ulp differences there as findings. An identity that holds
at one operating point and fails at another is the finding — sweep.
