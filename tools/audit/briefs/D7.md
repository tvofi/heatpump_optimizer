# D7 — Architecture and maintainability

Owner's brief: *"Architecture and maintainability — explicitly revisit
coordinator.py's size, the first-order sysid plant, the learner-freeze-versus-COP-flow
item, last_buffer_trajectory statefulness, and everything this year's train
introduced."*

Read `COMMON.md` first.

## Method

1. **Metrics, reproduced.** Lines, classes, methods per class, instance
   attributes and how many are assigned in more than one method, cyclomatic
   complexity per function, the import graph and its cycles. Own AST script;
   commit it.
2. **coordinator.py.** Cluster its methods by the `self._*` attributes they
   share (a greedy or spectral clustering over the attribute–method
   bipartite graph); report cross-cluster call and attribute fractions; name
   candidate seams with a measured cut cost (attributes and calls crossing).
   This feeds a decomposition program; give it numbers, not adjectives.
3. **The first-order sysid plant.** Generate step responses from the
   production `ThermalModel` for the three building presets; fit the
   production first-order identifier and a two-exponential fit; report the
   UA bias of each and whether the production adoption gate admits the
   biased fit.
4. **Learner freeze versus COP flow.** Enumerate every learner and the
   signal that gates its samples (open window, pump signals, external heat,
   defrost, away). Build a table; then simulate a contaminated interval and
   show, per learner, whether it ingests the sample.
5. **`last_buffer_trajectory`.** Construct the call order the objective and
   the terminal cost rely on; show what a reordering or a second objective
   call in between does to the terminal cost.
6. **This year's train.** For each addition since v4 (the batched gradient
   and its bounds gate, the drift gate, the scoped gate, the card
   collaborators, stress budgets, weekly windows, the topology catalogue,
   wood and coil variants, DHW confidence, Tuya, the re-anchor law):
   is there a test that fails when it is deleted (spot mutation), a doc
   paragraph, and one owning module? A table with executed spot-mutation
   results.
7. **Dead code**, by AST reachability confirmed with a runtime sentinel
   where a dynamic lookup could hide a use.

## Numbers

Everything above is a count or a ratio; timing is not your dimension.
